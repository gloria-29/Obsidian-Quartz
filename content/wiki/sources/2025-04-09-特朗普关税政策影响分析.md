### 核心要点

- Key Elements: The policy involves a 10% global baseline tariff on all imported goods, with higher, individualized reciprocal tariffs for countries with significant trade deficits with the US. Differentiated rates are applied to specific countries like Canada, Mexico, and China. Certain goods like pharmaceuticals, lumber, and bullion are exempted.
- Strategic Rationale: The policy aims to restore US manufacturing competitiveness, address non-reciprocal trade practices (currency manipulation, excessive VAT), and assert economic independence.
- Quote: \&quot;Restoring U.S. manufacturing competitiveness and re-shoring critical industries.\&quot; This highlights the core objective of bringing back manufacturing jobs to the US.
- Tariff Revenue and Import Volume: Projections estimate significant additional federal revenue (initially $100 billion per year, rising to $2.2 trillion by 2034). US imports are projected to decline by around 15%.
- Sector-Specific Price Increases: The report anticipates price increases in the automotive (up to $3,000 per vehicle), energy (up to 50 cents per gallon), and food sectors.
- GDP and Employment Implications: Analyst forecasts estimate a short-term GDP decline of 0.6 to 1 percentage point. Approximately 8 million US jobs are at risk due to retaliatory tariffs.
- Market Reactions and Currency Effects: US stock futures reacted negatively to the policy announcement. The Mexican peso is projected to depreciate by up to 30%, and the Canadian dollar by 8%.
- Comparative Historical Impact: The report draws parallels with the 2018 tariff rounds, suggesting initial market shocks followed by long-term economic restructuring.
- Table Summary: The report includes a table summarizing the estimated effects of the tariff policy on key metrics like US import decline, federal tax revenue, GDP growth, job impact, automotive price increase, and currency depreciation. This provides a concise overview of the quantitative analysis.
- Domestic and International Reactions: The policy has triggered domestic protests and political backlash, as well as serious concerns from key European, Canadian, and Asian leaders. Retaliatory measures from China, the EU, and Mexico are expected.
- Supply Chain and Sectoral Disruptions: The tariffs are expected to disrupt global supply chains, forcing businesses to reconsider sourcing strategies. Manufacturing, energy, automotive, high technology, agriculture, and food sectors face significant challenges.
- Broader Global Economic Implications: The OECD projects that the protectionist policies will dampen global economic growth. The unilateral approach risks exacerbating global trade tensions and may lead to a realignment in international trade rules.
