# OMAD Diet, Emotional Eating, and Health Resources

## Summary
A personal health support document addressing OMAD (One Meal A Day) challenges: morning low mood (likely blood sugar related), evening binge eating (emotional/stress-driven), and lack of mindfulness during meals. Recommendations span four categories—books ([[The Obesity Code]] by Jason Fung, [[Intuitive Eating]], [[The Body Keeps the Score]]), music (classical, meditation, ambient), films (Fed Up, Jiro Dreams of Sushi), and online resources (Mind Body Green, Healthline, mindful eating YouTube channels). Includes a disclaimer that OMAD is not suitable for everyone.
