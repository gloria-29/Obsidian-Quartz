# Committing to OMAD Lifestyle

## Summary
A personal commitment to restart [[OMAD]] (One Meal A Day), aiming to use the menstrual cycle window to achieve a target weight of 68kg. The plan: eat lunch as the single daily meal, maintain this pattern 9-to-5, and develop OMAD as a sustainable lifestyle rather than a temporary diet. References a book about making OMAD a lifestyle habit.

## Key Concepts
- [[OMAD]] (One Meal A Day) protocol
- Using physiological cycles (menstrual phase) for weight optimization
- Lifestyle formation vs. temporary dieting
