# Human System Optimization: Health to 150 Years

## Summary
A comprehensive guide synthesizing [[Huberman Lab]] neuroscience with longevity science ([[David Sinclair]]). Organized as an "owner's manual" for the human body covering sleep (circadian biology, light exposure protocols, temperature effects), diet (intermittent fasting with 8-hour eating window, Mediterranean diet > keto for sustainability, fermented foods for gut microbiome diversity), motivation/dopamine (limited dopamine reservoir, random intermittent reward, growth mindset turning effort into reward), learning (neuroplasticity requires focused attention + error signals, vestibular disruption triggers learning state), brain health (Omega-3 EPA, phosphatidylserine, creatine, blueberries), and longevity science (fasting extends lifespan 30%+, epigenome controls 80% of aging, Sinclair's personal protocol at age 52 yielding ~30-year-old biological age).

## Core Ideas
- **Sleep is the foundation**: morning sunlight exposure for 2-10 min sets circadian clock; evening light (11pm-4am) suppresses dopamine for days
- **Intermittent fasting (8hr window)**: activates autophagy, mTOR suppression, sirtuin activation—extends lifespan 30%+
- **Dopamine is a finite resource**: "random intermittent reward" prevents depletion; growth mindset turns effort itself into reward
- **Neuroplasticity requires errors**: the brain only rewires when detecting mistakes; vestibular disruption (novel balance challenges) triggers learning state
- **Fermented foods > fiber for microbiome diversity**: kimchi, kefir, natto, sauerkraut significantly outperform high-fiber diets for gut health
- **Epigenome controls 80% of aging**: fasting, exercise, and specific compounds (resveratrol, NMN, metformin) can reverse biological age
- **Longevity escape velocity predicted ~2034**: each year of science adds >1 year to average lifespan

## Key Concepts
- Melanopsin ganglion cells and circadian entrainment
- [[Autophagy]] and cellular cleanup through fasting
- mTOR vs. sirtuin pathways in aging
- [[Cold exposure]] for dopamine: 10-14degC water, 2.5x baseline dopamine for ~3 hours
- Physiological sigh for rapid stress reduction
- [[Yerba mate]] as recommended caffeine source
- EPA Omega-3 (1.5-3g/day) for brain health
- [[Yamanaka factors]] for cellular reprogramming
- Sinclair protocol: 1g resveratrol, 1g NMN, metformin, 2hr eating window, plant-based dinner
