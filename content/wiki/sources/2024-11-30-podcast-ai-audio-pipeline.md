# Podcast AI Audio Processing Pipeline

## Summary
A documented workflow for AI-powered audio processing of podcasts. The pipeline: (1) Download audio via Podcini or Seal, (2) Upload to processing tools ([[Cubox]], [[Dinox]], Tongyi, Baidu Wangpan, Nano Search), with secondary options (Summarizer, iMemo, PodQuest for transcription, [[NotebookLM]], Free Canvas). For direct listening: PodQuest, Spotify, YouTube/Lingvotube. Represents a personal knowledge management audio processing stack.

## Key Concepts
- AI audio transcription and summarization pipeline
- [[Cubox]] and [[Dinox]] as primary processing hubs
- [[NotebookLM]] for audio content analysis
- PodQuest for podcast transcription
- Multi-tool audio processing ecosystem
