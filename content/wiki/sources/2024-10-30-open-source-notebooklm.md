# Open Source NotebookLM Alternatives

## Summary
A brief list of open-source alternatives to [[NotebookLM]], Google's AI-powered research and note-taking assistant. Three options identified: [[Dify]] (open-source LLM app platform), [[Coze]] (pre-built bots platform), and Open NotebookLM (dedicated open-source alternative).
