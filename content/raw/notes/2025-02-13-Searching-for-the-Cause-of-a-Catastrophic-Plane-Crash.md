I—The Flight  我——航班
-------------------

![Image 22: An illustration of Thomas Haueter and Gregory Phillips in a collage filled with papers and airplanes.](https://github.com/gloria-29/OB-assets/releases/download/img-1b/1b3fcff882394b515520a61f699411628a07a91a0461ad6d48ca5cd5e0c80aa1.png)

The Safety Board’s Thomas Haueter and Gregory Phillips. The first challenge was to keep control of the investigation.Illustration by Steven Dana  
安全委员会的托马斯·豪特和格雷戈里·菲利普斯。第一个挑战是保持调查的控制权。史蒂文·达纳插图。

On the evening of Wednesday, September 7, 1994, a Boeing 737 jetliner owned by USAir arrived at Bradley International Airport, just outside Hartford, Connecticut, on a regularly scheduled flight.  
1994 年 9 月 7 日星期三晚上，美国航空公司的一架波音 737 客机在康涅狄格州哈特福德市外的布拉德利国际机场降落，这是一次定期航班。  
Before its departure early the next morning, for Syracuse, it underwent a transit check—a routine maintenance inspection that occurs every seven days or thirty-five flight hours, whichever comes first, on all commercial 737 jetliners in the United States.  
在第二天清晨出发前往叙拉古之前，它经历了一次过境检查——这是一项常规维护检查，每七天或飞行三十五小时，以先到者为准，适用于美国所有商用 737 喷气式客机。

This particular plane, a 737-300 registered as N513AU, was one of two hundred and thirty 737s operated by USAir. Boeing had manufactured it in October, 1987, and USAir had then bought it for approximately thirty million dollars.  
这架特定的飞机，一架注册号为 N513AU 的 737-300，是美国航空公司运营的 230 架 737 飞机之一。波音公司在 1987 年 10 月制造了它，美国航空公司当时以大约三千万美元的价格购买了它。  
During the previous seven years, it had logged nearly twenty-four thousand hours of flight time and more than fourteen thousand cycles, the term the industry applies to each takeoff and landing. As commercial airliners go, it was a relatively young plane.  
在过去的七年里，它已经累计飞行了近两万四千小时，超过一万四千个循环——这是行业对每次起飞和降落的术语。从商用客机的角度来看，它相对较新。  
With regular maintenance, it could be expected to provide decades of service.  
通过定期维护，可以预期其提供数十年的服务。

To all appearances, it was also in good health. The transit check, which consisted largely of a visual inspection of the aircraft and its fluid levels, revealed nothing untoward.  
表面上，它也处于良好状态。过境检查，主要是由对飞机及其液体水平进行视觉检查组成，没有发现任何异常。  
Mechanics had previously noticed several small defects—a dent in the left aft inboard flap assembly, worn duct sliders on both the right and the left engine’s thrust reversers, and a worn mount bushing on one thrust reverser—but the wear on these parts was considered within normal limits, and repair of them had been deferred until the plane’s next stopover at a maintenance center.  
机械师之前已经注意到几个小缺陷——左后缘襟翼组件上的一个凹痕、左右发动机推力反向器的磨损导轨滑块，以及一个推力反向器的磨损安装衬套——但这些部件的磨损被认为在正常范围内，并且修复已被推迟到飞机下次在维修中心的停留。  
The maintenance log had also noted “soft and spongy” flooring in the aisle next to Row 5. This, too, had been deferred, but USAir’s mechanics did perform a temporary repair by inserting a sheet of aluminum under the carpeting.  
维护日志还记录了 5 排旁边的走道“柔软且松软”的地板。这也被推迟了，但美国航空的机械师通过在地毯下插入一块铝板进行了临时修复。

On Thursday, September 8th, the last day of its existence, N513AU departed from Hartford at 6:20 _A_._M_. for the short flight to Syracuse. From there, it went to Rochester, then on to Charlotte, North Carolina. By noon, it was in Jacksonville, Florida.  
周四，9 月 8 日，存在期的最后一天，N513AU 于早上 6:20 从哈特福德起飞，进行了一次前往锡拉丘兹的短途飞行。从那里，它前往罗切斯特，然后飞往北卡罗来纳州的夏洛特。中午时分，它抵达了佛罗里达州的杰克逊维尔。  
Boeing had designed the 737, known variously in the airline community as the Guppy and as Fat Albert, for precisely these kinds of short and intermediate flights. It has proved to be a spectacularly successful design.  
波音设计了 737，在航空界被称为 Guppy 和 Fat Albert，正是为了这些短途和中途飞行。它已经证明是一个极其成功的机型设计。  
Since the plane’s first commercial flight, in 1968, Boeing has sold more than twenty-six hundred 737s worldwide—more than any other commercial jet transport ever built. The plane has a reputation for durability, reliability, and safety.  
自 1968 年首架波音 737 客机首飞以来，波音在全球范围内已售出超过 2600 架 737 系列飞机——比任何其他商用喷气式运输机建造的都要多。该飞机以耐用性、可靠性和安全性著称。  
Every hour, some five hundred 737s take off or land somewhere in the world.  
每小时，世界上大约有五百架 737 飞机起飞或降落。  
Nevertheless, like any highly sophisticated technological machine, it has not been without problems. Over almost three decades, sixty-seven have crashed, mostly in Third World countries; twenty-two have crashed since 1990.  
尽管如此，像任何高度复杂的技术机器一样，它并非没有问题。在近三十年的时间里，共发生了六十七起坠毁事件，大多发生在第三世界国家；自 1990 年以来，已有二十二起坠毁。  
But no single problem, in and of itself, had emerged as a critical safety issue.  
但没有任何单一问题本身成为关键安全问题。

In Jacksonville, N513AU had a change of flight crew. At 12:15 _P_._M_., Captain Peter Germano and his first officer, Charles Emmett III, arrived at the airport with three flight attendants and boarded the plane. Shortly before one o’clock, another USAir pilot, William Jackson, also got on board. Captain Jackson was not on duty during this flight.  
在杰克逊维尔，N513AU 更换了机组人员。下午 12:15，彼得·杰尔马诺上尉和他的副驾驶查尔斯·埃默特三世，带着三名乘务员抵达机场，登上了飞机。大约一点钟前，另一位美国航空公司飞行员威廉·杰克逊也上了飞机。杰克逊上尉在这趟航班上并未值班。  
He was deadheading, as such a ride is called, to Chicago, where he was scheduled to go on duty the following day. Upon boarding, he stopped at the cockpit door and introduced himself to Germano and Emmett.  
他正在执行所谓的“死头航班”，前往芝加哥，第二天他将在那里开始值班。登机后，他在驾驶舱门前停下，向 Germano 和 Emmett 介绍了自己。  
Then, since the plane was not fully booked, he took a seat in the passenger cabin.  
然后，由于飞机并未满座，他在乘客舱内找了个座位。

The seventy-minute flight back to Charlotte was uneventful.  
七十分钟的飞行返回夏洛特平静无奇。  
On the next leg, to Chicago’s O’Hare International Airport, paying customers occupied all the cabin seats, so Captain Jackson moved up into the cockpit, to the jump seat, a small, stiff, straight-backed chair situated behind the pilots’ center console.  
在下一程飞往芝加哥奥黑尔国际机场时，付费乘客占据了所有客舱座位，因此杰克逊机长移动到驾驶舱，坐在跳座上，这是一个位于飞行员中心控制台后面的又小又硬的直背椅。  
During the flight, he chatted with Germano and Emmett, whom he later described as “very capable and very professional.” He learned that they and the three flight attendants were on their final day of a three-day tour of duty that had taken them to half a dozen cities across the Eastern United States and Canada.  
在飞行过程中，他与 Germano 和 Emmett 聊天，后来他描述他们为“非常能干且非常专业。”他了解到他们和三名乘务员正在完成为期三天的最后一天任务，期间他们访问了美国东部和加拿大六个城市。  
Germano, who was forty-five, and lived in Moorestown, New Jersey, with his wife and two young children, had been flying for USAir since 1981. Emmett, thirty-eight, a tall, good-looking Texan who’d been married for less than two years, had flown commercial flights since 1987.  
德国诺，45 岁，住在新泽西州的摩尔顿，与妻子和两个年幼的孩子一起生活，自 1981 年以来一直在 USAir 公司飞行。埃米特，38 岁，一个高大英俊的德克萨斯人，结婚不到两年，自 1987 年以来一直在飞商业航班。  
Between them, Germano and Emmett had more than twenty-one thousand hours of flying experience, nearly eight thousand on 737s.  
他们两人加起来，Germano 和 Emmett 有超过两万一千小时的飞行经验，其中近八千小时是在 737 飞机上。

Midway through the flight to Chicago, after the plane attained cruising altitude, one of the flight attendants called Captain Germano on the intercom. A passenger in Row 1 had alerted her to an odd noise emanating from the ceiling of the cabin.  
飞行途中，飞机达到巡航高度后，一名乘务员通过内部通话器呼叫了 Germano 机长。1 排的一名乘客向她报告了来自客舱天花板的异常噪音。  
It sounded, the passenger later said, like “water gurgling . . . from a sink into a drain.” The flight attendant told Germano that she thought a noise was coming from the public-address system.  
它听起来，乘客后来表示，就像“水从水槽咕咕地流进排水管。”空姐告诉 Germano，她认为噪音来自公共广播系统。  
Germano turned to Jackson, seated in the cramped jump seat, and asked if he had inadvertently keyed the public-address microphone, which hung from the rear of the center console. Jackson had crossed his legs, and his knee had indeed brushed against the microphone.  
德国诺转向坐在狭小的跳座椅上的杰克逊，问他是否无意中按下了挂在中央控制台后部的公共广播麦克风。杰克逊交叉了双腿，他的膝盖确实碰到了麦克风。  
He moved his leg, and later reported that he’d heard no further complaint about unusual noises during the flight.  
他移动了腿，后来报告说他在飞行中再也没有听到过异常噪音的抱怨。

At O’Hare, Jackson said goodbye to his fellow-pilots and deplaned with the rest of the passengers. Germano and Emmett and the flight attendants were scheduled to depart in less than an hour, at 5:50 _P_._M_. Eastern Daylight Time, for a fifty-five-minute flight to Pittsburgh, their final destination on this tour of duty.  
在奥黑尔，杰克逊向他的同事们告别，与其他乘客一起下飞机。Germano、Emmett 和乘务员计划在不到一个小时后，即东部夏令时下午 5:50 起飞，进行一次 55 分钟的飞行，前往匹兹堡，这是他们这次任务旅程的最后一站。

Downstairs in the USAir maintenance office, Gerald Fox, a maintenance foreman, was about to leave for home when the phone rang. A woman on the other end told Fox that she was the wife of a passenger about to fly to Pittsburgh.  
美国艾尔航空维修办公室楼下，维修主管杰拉尔德·福克斯正准备回家，这时电话响了。电话那头的一个女人告诉福克斯，她是即将飞往匹兹堡的一名乘客的妻子。  
She’d overheard some passengers who had just got off the plane talking about strange noises they’d heard during the flight, and she was concerned for her husband’s safety.  
她无意中听到一些刚下飞机的乘客谈论他们在飞行中听到的奇怪噪音，她担心她丈夫的安全。  
Fox assured the woman that there were two USAir mechanics on duty who would take care of any problems before the plane departed for Pittsburgh.  
Fox 向那位女士保证，有两名 USAir 机务人员在值班，会在飞机前往匹兹堡之前处理任何问题。

After hanging up, Fox walked out to the jetway, where he encountered Captain Germano. He asked Germano about the status of the plane, and, as Fox later recalled, Germano replied that “there were no problems and everything was fine.” Satisfied with this, Fox left for home.  
挂断电话后，福克斯走到登机桥，在那里遇到了德国诺船长。他询问了飞机的状态，福克斯后来回忆说，德国诺回答说“没有问题，一切正常。”对此感到满意，福克斯回家了。

Now designated as Flight 427 to Pittsburgh, N513AU left the gate shortly after six o’clock E.D.T. with all but two seats occupied: a hundred and twenty-seven passengers were aboard, many of them business commuters, one a small child seated on his mother’s lap.  
现在被指定为飞往匹兹堡的 427 航班，N513AU 飞机在东部夏令时六点后不久离开登机口，除了两个座位外都已被占用：飞机上有 127 名乘客，其中许多是商务通勤者，还有一个坐在母亲腿上的小孩。  
Charles Emmett flew the plane on this leg of the trip while Germano handled the radio transmissions. It was an exceptionally fine day for flying. From Chicago to Pittsburgh, the sky was clear and virtually cloudless, with only light winds.  
查尔斯·埃米特在这段旅程中驾驶飞机，而德国诺处理无线电传输。那是一个飞行的好日子。从芝加哥到匹兹堡，天空晴朗，几乎无云，只有轻微的风。

Most of what is known about this flight comes from the flight-data recorder, which registers basic parameters such as airspeed, heading, and degree of pitch and roll, and from the cockpit voice recorder, a continuous loop of tape that records the pilots’ conversation and other cockpit sounds for thirty minutes and endlessly recycles itself.  
大多数关于这次航班的信息都来自飞行数据记录仪，它记录了基本参数，如空速、航向以及俯仰和滚转的角度，以及驾驶舱语音记录仪，这是一段连续循环的磁带，记录飞行员对话和其他驾驶舱声音，持续 30 分钟，然后无限循环。  
The voice recorder seemed to reflect an alert, capable, and congenial crew. Flying at cruise altitude on autopilot, Germano and Emmett did not have much to do aside from monitoring radio transmissions from air-traffic control.  
录音机似乎反映了警觉、能干和友好的机组人员。在巡航高度自动驾驶飞行，Germano 和 Emmett 除了监控来自空中交通控制的无线电传输外，没有太多事情要做。  
As they began their initial approach to Pittsburgh, they heard air-traffic control directing a Delta flight, a Boeing 727, some four and a half miles ahead of them, on the same flight path they would soon follow.  
他们开始接近匹兹堡时，听到空中交通管制正在指挥一架达美航空的波音 727 航班，这架飞机在他们前方大约四英里半，正沿着他们即将跟随的同一航线上。  
About seventeen minutes out of Pittsburgh, one of the flight attendants, a young woman of twenty-eight named Sarah Slocum-Hamley, opened the cockpit door and asked the pilots if they wanted anything to drink.  
大约在离开匹兹堡 17 分钟后，一位名叫莎拉·斯洛克姆-汉利的 28 岁年轻乘务员打开了驾驶舱门，询问飞行员们是否需要喝点什么。  
She offered to make them her “special fruity juice cocktail,” and both men accepted. “That is good,” Emmett said a few minutes later. “Be real good with some dark rum in it.”  
她提出要为他们制作她的“特别水果果汁鸡尾酒”，两个男人都接受了。“那很好，”埃米特几分钟 later 说。“放点黑朗姆酒会更好。”

Just about that time, Pittsburgh control instructed Flight 427 to descend to six thousand feet and throttle back to two hundred and ten knots. Emmett acknowledged the instructions.  
大约在那个时间，匹兹堡控制指令 427 航班下降至 6000 英尺并减小油门至 210 节。埃米特确认了指令。  
A minute later, he and Germano heard air-traffic control also instruct the Delta 727 to descend to six thousand feet. They began going through their checklist in preparation for landing.  
一分钟后，他和德国诺听到空中交通管制也指令 Delta 727 下降至六千英尺。他们开始检查清单，为降落做准备。

Then Emmett took the public-address microphone and said, in his Texas drawl, “Folks, from the flight deck, we should be on the ground in about ten more minutes. . . . Certainly ’preciate you choosing USAir for your travel needs this evening.  
埃米特拿起公共广播麦克风，用他的得克萨斯口音说：“各位，从飞行甲板来看，我们大约再过十分钟就能着陆了……当然非常感谢你们今晚选择美国航空来满足你们的旅行需求。”  
Hope you enjoyed the flight, hope you come back and travel with us again.” He asked the passengers to check their seat belts and told the flight attendants to prepare for arrival.  
希望您旅途愉快，希望您再次回来与我们同行。”他要求乘客检查他们的安全带，并告诉乘务员为到达做准备。

Air-traffic control instructed the Delta flight to turn left onto a heading of one zero zero. “Boy, they always slow you up so bad here,” Germano said. A minute and thirty-eight seconds later, the tower gave the same instructions to Germano and Emmett.  
空中交通管制指示达美航班向左转向一百度。 “哎呀，他们在这里总是让你减速减得这么厉害，”德国诺说。 一分三十八秒后，塔台向德国诺和埃梅特下达了同样的指令。

“That sun is gonna be just like it was taking off in Cleveland yesterday, too,” Emmett said. “I’m just gonna close my eyes.” He laughed. “You holler when it looks like we’re close.” He laughed again.  
那太阳会像昨天在克利夫兰起飞时一样，埃米特说。“我打算闭上眼睛。”他笑了。“当我们看起来很接近时，你就喊一声。”他又笑了。

Air-traffic control advised Flight 427 to be on the lookout for a northbound Jetstream, climbing up from three thousand three hundred to five thousand feet.  
空中交通管制建议 427 航班注意一架从三千三百英尺爬升至五千英尺的北行 Jetstream。

“We’re looking for traffic,” Germano replied.  
我们在寻找交通，”德国诺回答说。

Twenty seconds later, Emmett said, in a mock foreign accent, “Oh, ya, I see zuh Jetstream.”  
二十秒后，埃米特模仿外国口音说：“哦，是的，我看到了急流。”

At the instant Emmett began to articulate the word “Jetstream,” the cockpit voice recorder later revealed the sound of a muted thump. Two seconds after that, there came a sound similar to three electrical clicks. Germano muttered, “Sheez,” and Emmett grunted.  
埃米特开始发音“Jetstream”一词时，驾驶舱语音记录器后来揭示了沉闷的砰击声。两秒后，传来类似三次电击的声音。德国诺咕哝着，“我的天哪”，埃米特哼了一声。

There was another sound—a shuddering triple thump—followed half a second later by a rapid “clickety-click.” On the cockpit voice recorder, Germano can be heard inhaling and exhaling quickly, as if in surprise. Yet another thump, and the plane began a deep roll to the left.  
那里又传来另一种声音——一种颤抖的三次重击——半秒后跟着快速的“滴答滴答”声。在驾驶舱语音记录器上，可以听到 Germano 快速地呼吸，好像很惊讶。接着又是另一声重击，飞机开始向左深滚。

“Whoa!” exclaimed Germano.  
哇！德国诺惊呼道。

More clickety-clicks and the sound of the engine growing louder. “Hang on!” Germano cried to Emmett. “Hang on!”  
更多咔哒声，引擎声越来越大。“坚持住！”德国人对埃梅特喊道。“坚持住！”

Emmett, presumably wrestling with the steering column, can be heard grunting. The wailing horn of the autopilot disconnect began to sound.  
埃米特，大概在与方向盘作斗争，可以听到他发出哼哼声。自动驾驶断开时的尖锐警报声开始响起。

“Hang on!” Germano said again.  
“等等！”德国诺又说道。

Emmett swore.  埃米特发誓。

For nearly three seconds, neither pilot said a word, but Emmett’s breathing, from exertion or alarm or both, is clearly audible on the cockpit voice recorder.  
近三秒钟，两位飞行员都没有说话，但埃米特的呼吸，无论是由于劳累、惊慌还是两者兼而有之，在驾驶舱语音记录器上清晰可闻。

Then Germano exclaimed, “What the hell is this?”  
然后，Germano 惊呼，“这究竟是什么鬼？”

The cockpit became a symphony of alarms. The stick shaker—a menacing sound like the rattling of a large gourd filled with stones—went off, alerting the pilots that the plane had lost lift and was about to enter a stall.  
驾驶舱变成了警报交响曲。杆震器——一种像装满石头的葫芦摇动般威胁的声音——响了起来，警告飞行员飞机已经失去升力，即将进入失速状态。  
It was accompanied by a buffeting sound characteristic of a stall, by the musical tone of an altitude alert, and by a robotic warning voice repeatedly crying, “Traffic! Traffic!”  
它伴随着一种典型的滑流声，伴随着高度警报的音乐音调，以及一个反复喊叫的机器人警告声音，“交通！交通！”

“Oh, God! Oh, God!” said Germano.  
哦，上帝！哦，上帝！德国诺说。

The air-traffic controller in Pittsburgh, unaware of Flight 427’s plight, saw on his radar screen only that the plane had descended from its assigned altitude. He radioed the pilot to maintain six thousand feet.  
匹兹堡的空中交通管制员对 427 航班的不幸一无所知，他在雷达屏幕上只看到飞机已下降到指定高度。他通过无线电通知飞行员保持 6000 英尺的高度。

Germano responded by yelling to air-traffic control, “427 emergency!”  
德国诺回应道，对着空中交通管制大喊，“427 紧急情况！”

“Shit,” said Emmett.  “妈的，”埃米特说。

“Pull!” shouted Germano.  “拉！”德国诺喊道。

“Ohh, shit!” cried Emmett.  
“哦，见鬼！”埃米特喊道。

“Pull!” shouted Germano again.  
“拉！”德国诺又喊道。

“God!” said Emmett.  “上帝！”埃米特说。

Germano began a scream that would last the final one and a half seconds of the cockpit voice tape. In the fraction of a second before Flight 427, nearly vertical, hit the ground at a speed of three hundred and one miles an hour, Emmett uttered the word “No.”  
德国诺开始发出一声尖叫，这声尖叫将持续到最后一段驾驶舱语音记录的最后 1.5 秒。在 427 航班几乎垂直地以每小时三百零一英里的速度撞地之前的一瞬间，埃米特说出了“不”这个词。

Six miles away, at the Pittsburgh airport, air-traffic controller Richard Fuga stared in disbelief at his radar screen, on which Flight 427’s altitude readout was depicted as “XXX.” His voice rising in urgency, Fuga called repeatedly to 427 but got no response.  
六英里外，在匹兹堡机场，空中交通管制员理查德·富加难以置信地盯着他的雷达屏幕，屏幕上显示航班 427 的高度读数为“XXX”。他的声音在紧急中升高，富加反复呼叫 427，但没有任何回应。

Kenneth Erb, the air-traffic-control area manager, was just returning from a break when a traffic assistant rushed up to him and said, “We have a bad emergency.” Erb went quickly to Richard Fuga’s station.  
肯尼斯·厄布，空中交通管制区域经理，刚从休息中回来，这时一位交通助理匆匆跑来对他说：“我们这里有个紧急情况。”厄布迅速赶往理查德·富加的岗位。  
Within seconds, he had halted all arrivals and departures and instructed every controller to try to raise Flight 427 on all available frequencies. He called the tower cab and asked its occupants to scan the northwest sector for any sight of 427.  
几秒钟内，他停止了所有到达和出发，并指示每个控制员尝试在所有可用频率上呼叫 427 航班。他打电话给塔台驾驶室，要求其乘客扫描西北区域，看是否有 427 航班的出现。  
The tower reported a dense column of black smoke rising near the town of Aliquippa, in Hopewell Township. Erb’s first thought, later expressed to investigators from the National Transportation Safety Board, was that a bomb had gone off on board the plane.  
塔楼报告说，在阿利奎帕镇附近的霍普韦尔镇上空升起了一股浓密的黑烟。厄布的第一想法，后来向国家运输安全委员会的调查人员表达，是飞机上可能发生了炸弹爆炸。  
What else could have caused a 737 to fall like a stone out of the sky on a perfectly clear and serene evening?  
还有什么原因能让一架 737 在晴朗宁静的夜晚像石头一样从天空中坠落？

II—Where’s the Plane?  II—飞机在哪里？
--------------------------------

Thomas Haueter, a senior investigator with the National Transportation Safety Board, has two phone lines to his home in Great Falls, Virginia.  
托马斯·豪特，美国国家运输安全委员会的高级调查员，在弗吉尼亚州大瀑布的家中设有两条电话线路。  
On that Thursday evening, some seventeen minutes after Flight 427 went down, one phone rang, and as he reached to pick it up the other rang.  
在那周四晚上，飞机 427 坠毁后大约十七分钟，一部电话响了，当他伸手去接电话时，另一部又响了。  
The first caller, from the Federal Aviation Administration’s regional communications center, told Haueter of a report that a USAir Boeing 737 had crashed near Pittsburgh.  
第一位来电者，来自联邦航空管理局的区域通信中心，向豪特报告称，一架美国航空波音 737 飞机在匹兹堡附近坠毁。  
In the calls that followed in quick succession, Haueter learned that a skydiving aircraft in the vicinity had confirmed the accident, that the plane had gone down in a hilly, densely wooded area, and that local rescue teams were already on the scene.  
在随后的一系列通话中，豪特了解到附近的一架跳伞飞机已确认事故，飞机坠毁在多山的茂密森林地区，当地救援队伍已到达现场。

So, of course, was the news media. Within forty minutes of the crash, several Pittsburgh television stations had camera crews at the Green Garden Plaza shopping center, in Hopewell Township, near the site of the disaster, and CNN was soon broadcasting pictures of the scene.  
当然，新闻媒体也是如此。在坠机后的四十分钟内，几个匹兹堡电视台的摄制组已经抵达霍普韦尔镇的格林花园广场购物中心，该购物中心位于灾难现场附近，CNN 很快就开始播放现场的画面。

Commercial-airline disasters exert a unique fascination on the human mind, perhaps because they often involve large numbers of fatalities but also because flying, although statistically one of the safest means of modern transport, remains for many people a fundamentally unnatural act.  
商业航空灾难对人类心灵产生独特的吸引力，或许是因为它们往往涉及大量死亡，但也因为尽管从统计数据上看，飞行是现代最安全的交通方式之一，但对于许多人来说，飞行仍然是一种本质上不自然的行为。  
The airline industry, a multibillion-dollar sector of the economy, depends foremost on the travelling public’s perception that it is safe.  
航空业，作为经济中的数十亿美元产业，首先依赖于公众对其安全的认知。  
The National Transportation Safety Board is charged with investigating a wide range of commercial-transport accidents, but it is most visible when it deals with airline disasters.  
国家运输安全委员会负责调查各种商业运输事故，但在处理航空灾难时最为引人注目。  
It has a small staff—three hundred and fifty employees—and a thirty-eight-million-dollar budget, minuscule by Washington standards.  
它拥有一个小型员工团队——三百五十名员工——以及三千八百万美元的预算，按照华盛顿的标准来看微不足道。  
In its twenty-nine-year history, it has investigated three hundred and seventy-four major commercial-airline accidents (not counting the current T.W.A. Flight 800 investigation) and has found what it deems the “probable cause” in all but four.  
在其 29 年历史中，它调查了 374 起重大商业航空事故（不包括当前的 T.W.A.航班 800 调查），并在除四起之外的所有事故中找到了它认为的“可能原因”。  
Three of those four happened more than twenty-five years ago—ancient history in the aeronautical industry.  
这三件事中有四件发生超过 25 年前——在航空工业中是古代历史。  
Before Flight 427, the most recent and most troubling had concerned another Boeing 737, owned by United Airlines, which went down on March 3, 1991, killing everyone on board as it approached Colorado Springs.  
在飞行 427 之前，最近且最令人不安的事件涉及另一架由联合航空公司拥有的波音 737，该飞机于 1991 年 3 月 3 日坠毁，当时飞机正接近科罗拉多斯普林斯，机上所有人员均遇难。  
That investigation had dragged on for twenty-one months, then the longest and most costly ever undertaken by the Safety Board, and had closed without yielding any answer.  
那次调查持续了二十一个月，是安全委员会历史上耗时最长、成本最高的调查，但最终没有得出任何答案而结束。

Haueter had not worked on that accident. He recalls that it came briefly to mind on the night he learned about Flight 427, for both planes were 737s and both had crashed on approach.  
豪特未参与处理那起事故。他回忆说，在得知 427 航班当晚，那起事故也暂时浮现在他脑海中，因为两架飞机都是 737 型，且都在降落时坠毁。  
But each accident is unique, the result of countless variables, and Haueter consciously strove to avoid making any assumptions. “That’s one area that always causes trouble,” he likes to say.  
但是每起事故都是独特的，是无数变量的结果，Haueter 有意识地努力避免做出任何假设。“这是总引起麻烦的一个领域，”他喜欢说。  
In the months that followed, however, many people involved in the Flight 427 investigation, Haueter among them, began to see disturbing parallels between the two accidents.  
在随后的几个月里，然而，许多参与 427 航班调查的人，包括豪特，开始看到这两起事故之间令人不安的相似之处。

Haueter was forty-two, an aeronautical engineer with a graduate degree in management from George Mason University. He’d started flying, like his father and his grandfather before him, when he was fifteen.  
豪特尔四十二岁，是乔治·梅森大学管理专业的研究生，同时也是一名航空工程师。他像他的父亲和祖父一样，在十五岁时就开始了飞行。  
He had come to the Safety Board eleven years earlier as an analyst, evaluating responses to Safety Board recommendations, and had risen quickly through the ranks to become one of six investigators in charge—I.I.C.s, as they are called.  
他十一年前作为分析师加入安全委员会，评估安全委员会的建议响应，并迅速晋升，成为负责的六名调查员之一——他们被称为 I.I.C.。  
The I.I.C.s, along with a staff of specialists in a dozen disciplines, including power plants, aircraft structure, systems, operations, maintenance, meteorology, and human performance, all take turns in a weekly rotation on what is known as the Go-Team.  
国际核能机构（I.I.C.s）及其在电力设施、飞机结构、系统、运营、维护、气象和人类表现等十几个学科领域的专家团队，轮流参与被称为“Go-Team”的每周轮值。  
The team members, who are based both in Washington and at regional field offices, are on call twenty-four hours a day.  
团队成员，他们既在华盛顿，也在地区办事处，全天候待命。  
Most rotations pass uneventfully, with maybe one or two minor calls—an airliner mishap with no fatalities, or perhaps the crash of a privately owned Cessna—that don’t warrant a full Go-Team dispatch.  
大多数旋转都平安无事，可能只有一两个小插曲——一架客机事故没有造成人员伤亡，或者可能是一架私人拥有的塞斯纳飞机坠毁——这些都不需要派遣完整的 Go-Team。  
Haueter had known I.I.C.s who’d spent weeks, even months, at a time on rotation, hoping for a “major.”  
豪特曾认识一些 I.I.C.，他们一次轮换要花上几周甚至几个月的时间，希望能获得“重大突破”。

As it happened, Haueter hadn’t been scheduled for the Go-Team rotation the week of Flight 427. He had traded places with another I.I.C., who had requested the trade because his wedding anniversary fell during that week.  
实际上，Haueter 在 427 航班那周并没有被安排进入 Go-Team 轮换。他与另一位 I.I.C.交换了位置，那位 I.I.C.之所以要求交换，是因为他的结婚纪念日恰好在那一周。  
When, on the day after the crash, that I.I.C. offered to take over the investigation, Haueter told him, “It’s mine.” He said later, “This was one I was waiting to have happen.”  
在事故后的第二天，当 I.I.C.提出接管调查时，豪特告诉他：“这是我的。”他后来说：“这是我一直在等待发生的事情。”

One of Haueter’s closest friends at the Safety Board is Gregory Phillips, a specialist in flight-control systems and hydraulics. Although Haueter is nominally Phillips’s superior in the chain of command, they regard each other as equals.  
Haueter 在安全委员会最亲密的朋友之一是格雷戈里·菲利普斯，他是飞行控制系统和液压方面的专家。尽管在指挥链中 Haueter 在名义上是菲利普斯的上级，但他们彼此视为平等。  
They are about the same age, they both come from the Midwest (Haueter from Ohio, Phillips from Indiana), and they have worked together on many investigations—in Norway, in India, in Suriname, and, most recently, on the crash of another Boeing 737 into the Panamanian jungle.  
他们年龄相仿，都来自美国中西部（豪特来自俄亥俄州，菲利普斯来自印第安纳州），并且他们一起参与了多项调查——在挪威、在印度、在苏里南，以及最近一起调查了另一架波音 737 客机坠毁在巴拿马丛林的事故。  
Unlike Haueter, Phillips had worked on the Colorado Springs crash, and the failure to solve that one still ate at him. He had taken to monitoring every incident report, no matter how minor, that concerned flight-control anomalies on 737s.  
与 Haueter 不同，菲利普斯曾参与过科罗拉多斯普林斯空难的处理，而未能解决那起事件仍然让他耿耿于怀。他开始监控每一份有关 737 飞机飞行控制异常的任何事件报告，无论大小。  
On the evening of the Flight 427 crash, he was at home with his family, in Waldorf, Maryland. His youngest son, who was twelve, was watching television, and exclaimed, “Dad! There’s been a crash!” Phillips turned to CNN and watched for a few minutes.  
在 427 航班坠机当晚，他在马里兰州沃尔多夫的家中与家人在一起。他最小的儿子，12 岁，正在看电视，突然喊道：“爸爸！出事故了！”菲利普斯转向 CNN，看了一会儿。  
Colorado Springs did not leap immediately to his mind. His first thought was: Did it hit another plane? It had been in the vicinity of the airport, ready to land. He was on the Go-Team rotation that week, but even if he hadn’t been he would have helped with the investigation.  
科罗拉多斯普林斯并没有立刻出现在他的脑海中。他的第一个想法是：它是否击中了另一架飞机？它曾在机场附近，准备降落。他那一周在“行动小组”轮岗，即使他没有，他也会帮助进行调查。  
He had with him at home diagrams of the 737 and schematics of the hydraulic and flight-control systems. Before even making a phone call, he pulled a suitcase out of the closet and began to pack.  
他家里有 737 的图表和液压及飞行控制系统图。甚至在打电话之前，他就从衣柜里拉出一个箱子开始打包。

Planning for the logistics of the investigation went on throughout the evening and into the early hours of Friday.  
规划调查的物流工作一直持续到周五凌晨。  
Haueter and his superior at the Safety Board, Ronald Schleede, were forced to delay the Go-Team’s departure for Pittsburgh until just before dawn, until pilots for an F.A.A. plane became available. But it hardly mattered.  
豪特和他在安全委员会的上司罗纳德·施莱德被迫推迟 Go-Team 前往匹兹堡的出发时间，直到拂晓前，直到 F.A.A.飞机的飞行员可用。但这几乎无关紧要。  
Police and emergency crews in Hopewell Township had secured the perimeter of the crash site, and Haueter reasoned that in the darkness, in the middle of a forest, there was little that the Go-Team could hope to accomplish.  
霍普威尔镇的警察和紧急救援人员已封锁了事故现场周围，豪特认为在黑暗中，森林深处，Go-Team 能希望完成的事情很少。  
Haueter wanted two Safety Board specialists in each investigative group. He felt he’d need them to keep control of the investigation, which would also include teams of experts from Boeing, the engine manufacturer, USAir, the airline pilots’ union, and the F.A.A., among others.  
豪特想要每个调查小组都有两名安全委员会专家。他认为他们需要来控制调查，这还将包括来自波音（发动机制造商）、美国航空公司、飞行员工会以及联邦航空管理局（F.A.A.）等机构的专家团队。  
Simply finding a large conference room near the airport on short notice—one that could serve as Haueter’s command post and accommodate a hundred or more investigators for daily progress meetings—was proving difficult.  
简单地在短时间内找到机场附近的一个大型会议室——这个会议室可以充当 Haueter 的指挥所，并容纳一百名或更多调查员进行每日进度会议——正变得越来越困难。  
The news media were swarming into Beaver County, and hotel rooms were in short supply, too. By two o’clock, when Haueter lay down to get an hour or so of fitful sleep, he still did not know whether anyone on board Flight 427 had survived.  
新闻媒体蜂拥进入比弗县，酒店房间也供不应求。到了两点钟，当豪特尔躺下来试图睡上几个小时不安稳的觉时，他仍然不知道 427 航班上是否有人幸存。

Greg Phillips did not sleep at all. He went out to get some cash from an A.T.M. and to buy half a dozen extra rolls of film. Then he lay restlessly in bed, thinking about how to organize the investigation.  
格雷格·菲利普斯整夜未眠。他出去从自动取款机取了一些现金，并买了六卷额外的胶卷。然后他在床上辗转反侧，思考如何组织调查。  
At three o’clock, he got up, had breakfast, and left for Washington National Airport.  
三点钟，他起床，吃早餐，然后前往华盛顿国家机场。  
On his first major accident investigation—the 1988 crash of a Delta 727 on takeoff from Dallas–Fort Worth International Airport—he had been apprehensive about how he’d react to the sight of burned and mangled bodies. By the time he arrived, however, the bodies had been removed.  
在他第一次重大的事故调查——1988 年达美航空 727 客机在达拉斯-沃斯堡国际机场起飞时发生的坠机事故——他对看到烧焦和扭曲的尸体感到担忧。然而，当他到达时，尸体已经被移走了。  
On his next major—in 1989, of a DC-10 that had lost its hydraulics during flight and crashed in Sioux City, Iowa—he heard that the bodies were still strewn among the wreckage. In his Sioux City hotel room, the expectation of confronting death made him unable to sleep.  
在他的下一次重大事件中——1989 年，一架 DC-10 在飞行中失去了液压系统并在爱荷华州的苏城坠毁——他听说尸体仍散落在残骸中。在苏城的酒店房间里，面对死亡的预期让他无法入睡。  
He went out to the site early the next morning, and walked among the wreckage and the corpses. He did not feel, to his surprise, greatly distressed until he came upon the backpack of a small child, at a slight remove from the main body of the wreck. His knees went weak.  
他第二天一大早就去了现场，走在废墟和尸体之间。出人意料的是，他直到发现一个小孩子的背包，离废墟主体稍远一些，才感到极度痛苦。他的膝盖变得软弱无力。  
He felt, he said later, as if someone had hit him in the head. His own children carried similar backpacks when they flew to Phillips’s home town, in Indiana, every winter. He made it a point to find out what had happened to the child who’d owned the backpack.  
他后来表示，他感觉就像有人打了他头部一样。他自己的孩子们在每年冬天飞往印第安纳州的菲利普斯家乡时，都背着类似的背包。他特意去了解那个拥有背包的孩子发生了什么事。  
Of the two hundred and ninety-six people on the plane, a hundred and eleven had died. The child, Phillips learned later, was a boy and had survived.  
飞机上有 296 人，其中 111 人已死亡。菲利普后来得知，那个孩子是个男孩，并且幸存了下来。

The investigators convened before five-thirty on Friday morning at National Airport, where an F.A.A. Gulfstream waited to fly them to Pittsburgh. By now, Haueter knew that there were no survivors. Rumor had it that there were not even any intact bodies.  
调查人员在周五早上五点半之前在国家级机场集合，那里有一架 F.A.A.加勒德斯特姆飞机等待载他们飞往匹兹堡。此时，豪特已经知道没有幸存者。传言称甚至没有完整的尸体。  
Once airborne, Haueter sought out Carl Vogt, one of five Presidential appointees who headed the Safety Board. Vogt had served a two-year term as the board’s chairman, but he had never witnessed an investigation of this magnitude.  
一旦升空，豪特尔便寻找卡尔·沃格特，他是五位总统任命的领导安全委员会的五位成员之一。沃格特曾担任委员会主席两年，但他从未见证过如此规模的调查。  
“It’s going to be absolute chaos,” Haueter told him. “Give me time. Don’t think I’m screwing up for the first four or five hours.”  
“这将会是一片混乱，”豪特告诉他。“给我点时间。别以为我前四五个小时就搞砸了。”

It was dawn and was raining lightly when the Gulfstream landed in Pittsburgh. At the airport, the investigators were met by regional F.A.A. employees, who had gone to the crash site the night before and retrieved the flight-data recorder and the cockpit voice recorder.  
当时是黎明时分，当加勒比流飞机降落在匹兹堡时，下着小雨。在机场，调查人员遇到了地区级联邦航空管理局（F.A.A.）的员工，他们前一天晚上前往事故现场，取回了飞行数据记录器和驾驶舱语音记录器。  
The boxes—both dented, one of them breached by impact—were put aboard the Gulfstream for immediate delivery to the Safety Board’s labs in Washington.  
箱子——都有些凹陷，其中一个因撞击而破损——被装上湾流飞机，立即送往华盛顿安全委员会的实验室。  
Haueter had no idea how sophisticated 427’s flight-data recorder was, but he knew that it wouldn’t have a hundred or more parameters, as many 737s flying in European fleets did. “I knew I’d be lucky if it had thirty parameters,” he recalled thinking.  
豪特不知道 427 号飞机的飞行数据记录器有多么复杂，但他知道它不会有 100 个或更多的参数，就像在欧洲机队中飞行的许多 737 一样。“我知道如果它有 30 个参数我就很幸运了，”他回忆道。  
“It was possible it’d have only five”—like Colorado Springs—“and we’ve all seen enough flight-data recorders that didn’t work.”  
它可能只有五个——就像科罗拉多斯普林斯一样——我们所有人都见过足够多的无法工作的飞行数据记录仪。

The investigators drove in a caravan of rented cars to Hopewell Township. As they neared the Green Garden Plaza, a small shopping area nestled at the foot of a wooded hill, they saw in the early-morning light a great throng of people.  
调查员们驾驶着一辆租来的车队前往霍普韦尔镇。当他们接近位于树林山脚下的一个小型购物区——格林花园广场时，在清晨的阳光下，他们看到了一大群人。  
The night before, more than forty fire departments had responded to the crash.  
前一天晚上，超过四十个消防部门已响应了事故。  
Now jamming the plaza were police and fire vehicles, ambulances, television vans with satellite dishes, and the official cars of the Beaver County Coroner’s office and of the township, county, state, highway, and airport authorities.  
现在广场上挤满了警察和消防车辆、救护车、带有卫星天线的电视车，以及比弗县验尸官办公室和镇、县、州、公路和机场当局的官方车辆。  
Teams of clergy and mental-health and social workers had established a triage site to counsel bereaved family members, and also distraught witnesses and rescue personnel. The Salvation Army had set up a relief station to dispense food, drink, and blankets.  
神职人员、心理健康和社会工作者团队已设立一个分类站，为丧亲家属、惊慌失措的目击者和救援人员提供咨询。救世军已设立一个救济站，分发食物、饮料和毯子。  
Amid this crowd, more than a hundred members of the press clamored for scraps of information.  
在这群人中，一百多名记者争相获取信息碎片。  
Their numbers would grow as the day wore on, and the confusion would increase as the governor, the lieutenant governor, the Secretary of Transportation, and Dan Rather made their appearances.  
他们的数量会随着日子的推移而增加，随着州长、副州长、交通部长和丹·拉瑟的出现，混乱也会加剧。

As the investigator in charge, Haueter had the ultimate authority, by law, over all aspects of the crash investigation.  
作为负责人，豪伊特依法对事故调查的所有方面拥有最终权威。  
He and his team were greeted by police and rescue officials, who led them through the crowd to a temporary command post in the showroom of an automobile dealership. The crash site, Haueter learned, was half a mile away, just over the crest of the hill.  
他和他的团队受到了警察和救援官员的欢迎，他们带领他们穿过人群，来到一家汽车经销商展厅内的临时指挥所。Haueter 了解到，事故现场在半英里之外，就在山丘的顶峰上方。  
A crude dirt road ran nearby.  
附近有一条崎岖的土路。

The Safety Board team, in the company of emergency-response personnel, made the trip up the hill in four-wheel-drive vehicles. The intermittent morning rain had let up for the moment, and an ethereal mist draped the hills. The vehicles stopped at the edge of a pine grove.  
安全委员会团队与应急响应人员一起，乘坐四驱车上了山。间歇性的晨雨暂时停歇，山丘被一层空灵的薄雾笼罩。车辆停在一片松树林的边缘。  
Investigators say that aircraft accidents all have the same distinctive smell, and that once experienced it is never forgotten.  
调查人员表示，飞机事故都有一种独特的气味，一旦经历过就永远不会忘记。  
When Haueter stepped out of the vehicle, he could smell the reek of this accident—the jet fuel, the smoldering remains of the plane, the scent of roasted flesh—even though he could not yet see it. Up here, near the site of the accident, there were no crowds.  
当豪特走出车辆时，他能闻到这场事故的恶臭——喷气燃料、飞机燃烧后的残留物、烤肉的气味——尽管他还看不见。在这里，事故现场附近，没有人群。  
The investigators began walking as a group among the pines, but within a few moments each proceeded as if alone.  
调查员们开始成群地在松树间行走，但几分钟后，每个人都仿佛独自一人般继续前行。  
They saw hanging in the branches of the trees debris of every sort—charred pieces of paper, scraps of aluminum, shreds of cloth and plastic, and bits of insulation, wiring, and human flesh.  
他们看到树枝上挂着各种碎片——烧焦的纸片、铝片、布和塑料碎片，以及绝缘材料、电线和人体组织。  
Drawing closer to the site of impact, Haueter came upon a femur stripped clean of muscle and sinew. All around him, amid the fragments of the aircraft, remnants of bone, flesh, and viscera lay on the forest floor. Mostly, they were unrecognizable as parts of human bodies.  
随着越来越接近撞击地点，豪特发现了一根被肌肉和肌腱彻底剥离的股骨。在他周围，飞机的碎片、骨骼、肉体和内脏的残留物散落在森林的地面上。大多数情况下，它们无法辨认出是人体的部分。  
In more than two dozen major investigations, Haueter had never seen such carnage. It posed a clear biohazard, and that in itself posed a minor impediment to the investigation.  
在超过二十多项主要调查中，豪特从未见过如此惨状。这构成了一个明显的生物危害，而这本身对调查造成了一定的阻碍。  
Everyone who came onto the site thereafter, Haueter decided, would have to wear a full bodysuit and mask as a safeguard against infectious diseases.  
此后进入该场所的每个人，Haueter 决定，都必须穿着全身服和口罩，作为预防传染病的保障。

The investigators came out of the forest and stood on the edge of an embankment that sloped steeply down to a dirt road. Below them, in a great gouge of earth on the opposite embankment, was the impact site of Flight 427.  
调查人员走出森林，站在一条陡峭倾斜至土路的堤坝边缘。在他们下方，在对岸堤坝的一个巨大的土坑中，是 427 航班的事故现场。

The majority of all aircraft accidents are “hit and skips.” Wings may shear off, fuselages may break apart, yet the wreckage is identifiable as an airplane even to a layperson. But the fragments of Flight 427, still smoking from fire, bore no resemblance to a craft of any sort.  
大多数飞机事故都是“击中后跳过。”机翼可能会脱落，机身可能会断裂，但即使对普通人来说，残骸也能被识别为飞机。但 427 航班的碎片，仍然冒着烟，与任何类型的飞行器都不相似。  
There was, one investigator later observed, scarcely a piece larger than a car door.  
那里几乎没有一块比汽车门大的。  
Looking down the embankment, Cynthia Keegan, a specialist in aircraft structures, who was responsible for documenting every fragment of the wreckage and determining the structural integrity of the plane at the time of the accident, said to herself, “My God, where’s the plane?”  
向堤岸望去，飞机结构专家辛西娅·基根，当时负责记录每一块残骸并确定事故时飞机的结构完整性，自言自语道：“我的天哪，飞机在哪里？”

The investigators made their way cautiously down the soft earth of the embankment. Amid the wreckage, they began identifying parts of the plane—the shock strut of a landing gear, a wing spar, the smoldering hulk of an engine.  
调查员小心翼翼地沿着河堤柔软的泥土下行。在废墟中，他们开始识别飞机的部件——起落架的减震支柱、机翼梁，以及一个正在冒烟的发动机残骸。  
The largest identifiable portion of the plane, although severely damaged by impact forces and fire, was the empennage—the tail section, consisting of the rudder and the vertical and horizontal stabilizers.  
飞机上可识别的最大部分，尽管受到撞击力和火灾的严重破坏，是尾翼——由舵面和垂直尾翼及水平尾翼组成。  
Clearly the plane had hit nose first, the cockpit and fuselage disintegrating as they absorbed the great impact of the crash. The team found what little remained of the cockpit. “You look for the largest group of wires,” Greg Phillips said. “A bird’s nest of wires.”  
显然飞机是先撞到鼻部的，驾驶舱和机身在吸收巨大的撞击力时解体。团队找到了驾驶舱残留的少许部分。“你们去找最大的那团电线，”格雷格·菲利普斯说。“就像鸟巢一样的一团电线。”

The recovery of body parts from the wreckage, the first task of the coroner’s team, concerned Phillips. The job would obviously require a large number of emergency-rescue personnel, and Phillips did not want anyone muscling around pieces of the plane just yet.  
从残骸中恢复身体部位，法医团队的首要任务，让菲利普斯感到担忧。这项工作显然需要大量紧急救援人员，而菲利普斯不想任何人现在就随意搬动飞机碎片。  
“I figured body-part removal could impact the wreckage. I didn’t want it disrupted. . . . If we can figure out what happened in the first hour, then we know what area has the highest probability of paying off.”  
我估计身体部位移除可能会影响残骸。我不想它被打扰……如果我们能弄清楚前一个小时发生了什么，那么我们就知道哪个区域最有可能带来回报。

Some months later, Haueter recalled his thoughts as he surveyed the scene around him: “Even if you know what caused a crash, the investigation goes forward—you still have to fill in each box—but it’s focussed.  
数月后，豪特在环顾四周时回想起了自己的想法：“即使你知道了事故的原因，调查还是会继续——你仍然需要填写每一个方框——但它是专注的。”  
But I knew that even if this was a simple accident it was going to be difficult, given the degree of destruction. Even if the flight-data recorder and the voice recorder told us exactly what had happened, it was going to be difficult.  
即使这只是一次简单的意外，鉴于破坏程度，它也将是困难的。即使飞行数据记录器和语音记录器告诉我们确切发生了什么，它也将是困难的。  
This was not going to be four days on the scene and we all go home. This was going to be weeks.”  
这不是要在现场待四天然后我们都回家。这将是几周。”

On their first trip in, the Safety Board investigators did not linger long at the crash site.  
在他们的第一次调查中，安全委员会的调查员没有在事故现场逗留太久。  
Haueter had scheduled an organizational meeting at ten o’clock with all the parties to the investigation, for by then teams of crash experts from Boeing and the other parties would have arrived.  
豪特尔已经安排了十点钟与所有调查方的组织会议，因为那时波音和其他方的坠机专家团队将已到达。  
The Safety Board had not been able to find a room large enough for this first meeting. USAir, which has a large facility in Pittsburgh, offered one of its executive conference rooms. With reluctance, Haueter accepted.  
安全委员会未能找到足够大的房间来举行这次首次会议。美国航空公司，在匹兹堡拥有大型设施，提供了一间其行政会议室。Haueter 不情愿地接受了。  
He did not like using the facilities of a party to the crash, but for the moment he had little choice.  
他不喜欢利用事故方的设施，但此刻他别无选择。

The appearance of an independent, objective inquiry into the cause of an accident is crucial to the Safety Board’s credibility. Yet the Board does not have the manpower or the expertise to conduct a major investigation on its own.  
独立、客观的事故原因调查对安全委员会的信誉至关重要。然而，委员会没有足够的人力和专业知识自行开展重大调查。  
It relies on what it refers to as “the party system,” calling upon experts from the interested parties to assist in the investigation, on the premise, for example, that no one knows the working innards of a General Electric jet engine better than the General Electric engineers who designed and built that engine.  
它依赖于所谓的“政党体系”，召集相关方的专家协助调查，例如，在这样一个前提下，没有人比设计并制造通用电气喷气发动机的通用电气工程师更了解该发动机的内部运作。  
These experts are assigned to Safety Board groups—in this case, Boeing structural engineers to the structures group, USAir maintenance personnel to the maintenance group, members of the pilots’ union to the human-performance and other groups—and each group, of anywhere from five to ten or more members, is chaired by a Safety Board specialist. Given the money, the reputations, and the potential exposure to liability that are at stake in determining the cause of a crash, the conflicts of interest in such a system seem clear and incurable.  
这些专家被分配到安全委员会小组——在这种情况下，波音的结构工程师被分配到结构小组，USAir 的维护人员被分配到维护小组，飞行员协会成员被分配到人机性能和其他小组——每个小组，成员人数从五到十个或更多，都由安全委员会的专家担任主席。鉴于确定事故原因所涉及的金钱、声誉以及潜在的赔偿责任，这种系统中的利益冲突似乎明显且无法治愈。  
Haueter recalls his own astonishment when, newly arrived at the Safety Board, he learned about the system: “I said, ‘You’ve got to be kidding. That can’t possibly work.’ But I found out that it did work. There are problems, of course, and some very heated debates at times.  
豪特回忆起自己刚到安全委员会时对系统的惊讶：“我说，‘你们肯定是在开玩笑。这不可能行得通。’但后来我发现它确实行得通。当然，有问题，有时还会有些非常激烈的辩论。”  
People would like to drive the investigation in their own interest. It’s up to me to control that.”  
人们希望根据自己的利益来推动调查。这取决于我控制。”

Haueter’s primary instrument of control lay in his authority to dismiss any fractious party member from the investigation. He made this authority known when he called to order the organizational meeting in USAir’s conference room.  
豪特的主要控制手段在于他有权将任何桀骜不驯的党派成员从调查中开除。当他下令在 USAir 会议室召开组织会议时，他宣布了这项权力。  
Two hundred people had crowded into the large room, some of them sitting on the floor, others spilling out through the doors. A number of them, predictably, were USAir executives.  
两百人挤进了大房间，其中一些人坐在地板上，其他人从门口涌出。不出所料，其中一些人是 USAir 的行政人员。

Standing before them, Haueter began to lay out the rules of the investigation. Haueter is six feet tall and has regular features that many would call handsome.  
站在他们面前，豪特开始阐述调查规则。豪特身高六英尺，五官端正，许多人会称他长得英俊。  
He has a full head of reddish-blond hair and eyes as blue as any movie star’s, and he is trim, although not especially athletic. He left the Midwest long ago, but he still retains the polite, agreeable social manner often attributed to Midwesterners.  
他有一头红棕色的头发，眼睛像电影明星一样蓝，身材苗条，尽管不是特别健壮。他很久以前就离开了中西部，但仍然保持着通常被认为属于中西部人的礼貌、令人愉快的社交方式。  
Beneath that manner, he has an uncommonly sweet nature. In the largely male world of the aeronautical community, this trait is not always held in especially high regard. But Haueter got things done, by consensus, polite persistence, and engineering probity.  
在这种方式下，他有着不同寻常的甜美性格。在以男性为主的航空界，这种特质并不总是受到特别高的重视。但豪特通过共识、礼貌的坚持和工程诚信完成了事情。  
Lacking charisma, he also seemed to lack guile, and this, in turn, inspired trust and loyalty among those who worked with him.  
缺乏魅力，他似乎也缺乏狡猾，而这反过来又在他共事的人中激发出信任和忠诚。

The organizational meeting lasted two hours, until shortly after noon. As Haueter had warned Carl Vogt, it was a chaotic affair, complicated by the sheer number of people and by whispered undercurrents of information and rumor.  
组织会议持续了两个小时，直到中午过后不久。正如 Haueter 曾警告卡尔·沃格特的那样，这是一场混乱的事件，人数众多和信息、谣言的暗流涌动使得情况更加复杂。  
Haueter told the crowd that only those people he himself authorized would be permitted at the crash site and at all future meetings.  
豪特对人群说，只有他自己授权的人才能被允许进入事故现场和所有未来的会议。  
He could not allow the presence of public-relations, marketing, insurance, or legal counsel for any of the parties, or anyone else who did not have an essential role in the investigation.  
他不能允许任何一方或任何其他没有在调查中扮演关键角色的人参与公关、营销、保险或法律咨询。  
He introduced the Safety Board group chairmen and then went around the room asking each party in turn whom they would have serve on a given group.  
他介绍了安全委员会的主席，然后绕着房间走，依次询问每个党派他们希望谁加入某个小组。  
The investigators were issued identification badges that would gain them entry to the crash site and to the daily progress meetings, to be held at Haueter’s command post at six o’clock every evening.  
调查员被发放了识别证，这使他们能够进入事故现场和参加每天晚上六点在豪伊特的指挥所举行的进度会议。

As the organizational meeting drew to a close, the investigative groups convened among themselves and then departed on their separate missions. Some went to the crash site.  
随着组织会议的结束，调查小组相互召集并分别出发执行各自的使命。一些人前往事故现场。  
Others headed off to the airport to inspect the radar record of 427’s flight path, to USAir facilities to examine maintenance records, and, later, to Chicago to interview air-traffic controllers, eyewitnesses, and passengers there from the preceding flight.  
其他人前往机场检查 427 航班飞行路径的雷达记录，前往 USAir 设施检查维护记录，后来还前往芝加哥采访空中交通管制员、目击者和前一次航班的乘客。

Haueter went directly to a Holiday Inn near the airport, where the Safety Board had finally commandeered a large conference room. The room was being equipped with computers, fax machines, and twenty phone lines.  
豪特直接去了机场附近的一家假日酒店，安全委员会最终占领了一个大会议室。这个房间正在配备电脑、传真机和 20 条电话线。  
It would serve as Haueter’s command post, the place he would inhabit for eighteen hours a day in the coming week.  
它将成为 Haueter 的指挥所，他在接下来的一个星期里将在这里居住 18 个小时。

By late morning, the flight-data recorder and the cockpit voice recorder had arrived at the Safety Board’s labs in Washington.  
上午晚些时候，飞行数据记录器和驾驶舱语音记录器已抵达华盛顿安全委员会的实验室。  
There, an electronics engineer named James Cash labored with several colleagues for half an hour to cut open the three-eighths-inch stainless-steel jacket that encased the Fairchild voice recorder.  
在那里，一位名叫詹姆斯·卡什的电子工程师与几位同事一起努力了半小时，才切开包裹着 Fairchild 语音记录器的 3/8 英寸不锈钢外壳。  
The recorder, about the size of a shoebox, had been mounted in the aft cargo compartment. The impact of the crash had compressed the entire unit by three inches and destroyed the circuitry of the recorder. “It took a pretty good hit,” Cash said.  
录音机，大约鞋盒大小，已被安装在货舱后部。撞击的冲击力使整个装置压缩了三英寸，并摧毁了录音机的电路。“它受到了相当大的撞击，”Cash 说。  
The steel memory module, which contained the four-channel tape, was also dented. The tape itself, however, appeared undamaged.  
钢制内存模块，其中包含四通道磁带，也被压凹了。然而，磁带本身看起来没有损坏。

In his soundproof lab, Cash mounted the tape. Ron Schleede, the head of the Major Investigations Division, and several other Safety Board people began listening to it from the beginning.  
在他的隔音实验室里，Cash 安装了磁带。大调查部门负责人 Ron Schleede 和几位安全委员会的人从开头开始听。  
Cash had worked at the Safety Board for twelve years, and had heard nearly five hundred accident tapes.  
现金在安全委员会工作了十二年，几乎听过了五百个事故录音带。  
He recalls vividly his reaction to the first one—a twin-engine turboprop, a Convair, landed in a snowbank; a propeller blade came off and pierced the cabin, killing a woman—but all others since then have left him largely unaffected.  
他清晰地记得自己对第一架飞机的反应——一架双引擎涡桨飞机，康维尔公司制造的，降落在雪堆中；一个螺旋桨叶片脱落并刺穿机舱，导致一名妇女死亡——但此后所有其他事件都对他影响不大。  
He listens to their content with professional dispassion. “It doesn’t bother me much anymore to listen to them,” he says. “Every once in a while, you get one that’s unnerving.  
他带着专业的冷静聆听他们的内容。“再听他们说话已经不再让我那么烦恼了，”他说。“偶尔会遇到一些让人不安的。”  
I can tell the ones that will affect the other listeners—crew and airline people who knew the people in the crash. We’ve had them cry in here. Colorado Springs, there was a woman co-pilot, but the recording quality was very bad.”  
我能分辨出那些会影响其他听众的人——知道坠机者的人，船员和航空公司人员。我们让他们在这里哭过。科罗拉多斯普林斯，有一位女副驾驶，但录音质量非常差。”

Cash did not find the cockpit voice recording of Flight 427 unnerving in the least. As accident tapes went, it was strictly ordinary, with one significant exception: it was perhaps the best-quality major-accident tape that Cash had ever heard.  
现金觉得 427 航班驾驶舱语音记录并不令人不安。就事故录音而言，它非常普通，只有一个显著例外：这可能是现金听过的质量最好的重大事故录音。  
The tape itself was not brand-new, but both pilots had been wearing headsets with boom microphones.  
磁带本身并非全新，但两名飞行员都戴着带有话筒杆的头戴式耳机。  
(In Cash’s experience, most commercial airliners built before 1992 came equipped only with handheld mikes.) And along with the boom mikes and the cockpit-area microphone, situated in the overhead instrument panel, Cash discovered a fourth channel: the microphone in the jump-seat oxygen mask, which had been left on, no doubt inadvertently.  
在凯什的经验中，大多数在 1992 年之前建造的商用客机只配备了手持麦克风。）此外，在机载麦克风和驾驶舱区域麦克风以及位于头顶仪表板上的麦克风一起，凯什发现了一个第四通道：跳座椅氧气面罩上的麦克风，无疑是不小心留下的。

Listening to the tape, Cash heard a sound he did not recognize—the sound of the muted thumps that had occurred in quick succession near the moment of upset. They did not particularly stand out above the ambient noise of the cockpit, but Cash caught them on first hearing.  
听着磁带，凯什听到了一个他不认识的声音——在混乱时刻附近连续发生的沉闷的砰砰声。这些声音并没有在驾驶舱的背景噪音中特别突出，但凯什第一次听到时就注意到了。  
He said to Ron Schleede, “I think they hit something.”  
他说：“我觉得他们撞到什么了。”

They listened to the final thirty seconds many times. Cash thought that if Flight 427 had indeed hit something—perhaps a bird—it had struck the plane somewhere behind the nose. “If it was the cockpit or the radome, you’d hear wind noise.”  
他们多次听了最后三十秒。卡什认为，如果 427 航班确实撞到了什么东西——可能是一只鸟——它是在飞机鼻子后面撞上的。“如果是驾驶舱或雷达罩，你会听到风声。”

Ron Schleede entertained the idea of a bomb. “It wouldn’t have surprised me,” he recalled some time later. “But it could also have been just a toilet seat falling down with a bang, or a flight attendant putting a bin away.”  
罗恩·施莱德曾考虑过炸弹的可能性。“这不会让我感到惊讶，”他后来回忆道。“但这也可能只是马桶座掉下来发出巨响，或者是一位空姐把垃圾桶放回原位。”

The tape contained a vast amount of information beyond what was merely audible to Cash and Schleede. A sound-spectrum analysis, for example, would later reveal whether the thumps bore the characteristic acoustical signature of an explosion.  
磁带包含了超出 Cash 和 Schleede 仅能听到的大量信息。例如，声音频谱分析后来会揭示这些撞击声是否具有爆炸的特征声学标记。  
But it would take time for Cash to extract this information. Schleede had hoped for something more immediate. He’d hoped that the pilots might have actually told him what had happened, but they themselves obviously hadn’t known.  
但是 Cash 提取这些信息需要时间。Schleede 原本希望得到更及时的信息。他原本希望飞行员能告诉他发生了什么，但他们显然自己也不知道。  
“An airplane like that doesn’t just fall out of the sky on a clear evening,” Schleede said. “It sounded a lot like Colorado Springs—unexpected. Everything’s fine, and then boom! They’re fighting the airplane, fighting to control it.”  
“那样的飞机不可能在晴朗的夜晚从天而降，”施莱德说。“听起来很像科罗拉多斯普林斯——出乎意料。一切都很好，然后砰！他们正在与飞机搏斗，试图控制它。”

Schleede reached Haueter at two o’clock on Friday afternoon at the command post at the Holiday Inn, where the phones had just been installed.  
施莱德周五下午两点在假日酒店的指挥中心与豪特尔会面，那里刚刚安装了电话。  
He told Haueter about the thumps and advised checking for a bird strike, most likely Canada geese—one of the few birds the plane might have encountered at six thousand feet. Beyond that, Schleede said, the cockpit voice recorder wasn’t going to provide any immediate answers.  
他告诉豪特关于撞击声，并建议检查是否发生了鸟击，很可能是加拿大鹅——飞机在六千英尺高空可能遇到的少数几种鸟类之一。此外，施莱德说，驾驶舱语音记录器不会提供任何立即的答案。

The bomb theory, heretofore purely speculative, gained currency when Haueter learned from the F.B.I. on Friday afternoon that one of the passengers on Flight 427 had gone to Chicago at the request of the United States Attorney’s office.  
炸弹理论，此前纯粹是推测性的，在豪特从 FBI 那里得知，427 航班上的一名乘客应美国检察官办公室的要求前往芝加哥后，开始流行起来。  
Initial speculation had it that this passenger, a thirty-four-year-old man named Paul Olson, was in the federal witness-protection program. This speculation proved false, but it persisted as rumor.  
最初猜测认为，这位名叫保罗·奥尔森的 34 岁男子正在联邦证人保护计划中。这种猜测被证明是错误的，但它仍然作为谣言流传。  
Olson had in fact been summoned only to provide information, and perhaps to testify, in a federal drug case. His fiancée told the press that he had been nervous about testifying, but the United States Attorney’s office asserted that Olson was a “minor” witness.  
奥尔森实际上只是被传唤来提供信息，也许是要作证，在一宗联邦毒品案件中。他的未婚妻告诉媒体，他对于作证感到紧张，但美国检察官办公室声称奥尔森是一个“次要”证人。

Up at the crash site, investigators laid out a grid dividing the site into quadrants.  
在事故现场，调查人员划出一个网格，将现场划分为四个象限。  
By early afternoon, the structures group began mapping and photographing the distribution of the wreckage within the quadrants while the coroner’s deputies and some emergency-service volunteers fixed small red pennants next to each body part.  
到下午早些时候，结构组开始绘制和拍摄各个象限内残骸的分布情况，同时法医助理和一些紧急服务志愿者在每一部分尸体旁边固定了小红旗。  
In a matter of hours, hundreds of pennants, like a forest of red seedlings, covered the terrain. Everyone who entered the site was told to wear a full bodysuit and mask and, on leaving, to undergo decontamination in a solution of bleach and water.  
数小时内，数百面旗帜，如同一片红苗林，覆盖了这片土地。进入现场的人都被告知要穿上全身防护服和口罩，离开时需在漂白水和水的溶液中进行消毒。  
Biohazard regulations also called for the decontamination of any pieces of wreckage showing traces of blood or body tissue before they could be removed to a nearby USAir hangar.  
生物危害法规还要求在将任何带有血迹或人体组织的残骸移至附近的 USAir 机库之前，对这些残骸进行消毒。

Three decontamination stations were designed and built at the perimeter of the site with the help of Edward Kittel, a specialist from the F.A.A.’s Aviation Explosives Security Unit. Kittel’s real task, however, was to examine the wreckage for signs of a bomb.  
三个去污站在该场所外围设计并建造，在 F.A.A.航空爆炸物安全单位专家爱德华·基特尔的帮助下完成。然而，基特尔的真正任务是检查残骸以寻找炸弹的迹象。  
(He is now working on the T.W.A. Flight 800 crash.) Even before hearing about Paul Olson, Kittel had regarded this crash as suspicious.  
(他现在正在调查 T.W.A.航班 800 号坠机事件。)甚至在听到保罗·奥尔森的事之前，基特尔就已经认为这次坠机可疑。  
“It didn’t happen on takeoff or landing, which is when airplanes typically crash, and the weather was fine.” Moreover, one eyewitness, a USAir employee, had reported seeing reddish-brown smoke coming out of the right side of the plane, forward of the wing.  
它没有发生在起飞或降落时，这是飞机通常坠毁的时候，而且天气很好。此外，一名目击者，一名 USAir 员工，报告说看到从飞机右侧、机翼前方冒出红棕色的烟雾。  
Kittel generally found eyewitness reports less reliable than other evidence, but this one had come from an airline employee, and the smoke—if there had indeed been smoke—might have come from the forward cargo hold, a likely area for a bomb.  
基特尔通常认为目击报告不如其他证据可靠，但这次报告来自一名航空公司员工，而且如果确实有烟雾，那么烟雾可能来自前面的货舱，那里是炸弹的可能放置区域。  
As a rule, a plane’s tail and wings held little attraction for Kittel; cargo holds and doors, which might blow out in an explosion, interested him most. During the next week and a half, he would examine thousands of fragments of wreckage.  
一般来说，飞机的尾巴和机翼对基特尔吸引力不大；货舱和可能因爆炸而吹出的舱门，才是他最感兴趣的。在接下来的一个半周里，他将检查数千块残骸碎片。  
All high explosives, from the crudest ammonium-nitrate-and-fuel-oil bomb to the most sophisticated plastics, like Semtex, detonate at rates ranging from thirty-three hundred to around twenty-five thousand feet or more per second.  
所有高爆炸药，从最粗糙的硝酸铵和燃料油炸弹到最复杂的塑料，如塞姆特克斯，其爆炸速度从每秒三千三百英尺到约两万五千英尺或更多。  
This force creates distinctive blast pits and craters, along with soot deposits and gas washes that leave radial streaks on nearby surfaces.  
这股力量形成了独特的爆破坑和陨石坑，以及留下附近表面径向条纹的烟灰沉积和气体冲刷。  
Kittel’s tools for finding these signs were, as he described them, “low tech”: a magnifying glass, a magnet, and cotton swabs and containers for collecting residue samples.  
基特尔寻找这些迹象的工具，正如他描述的那样，“低技术”：一个放大镜、一个磁铁、棉签和收集残留样品的容器。

The removal of body parts began late on Friday afternoon. Wayne Tatalovich, the county coroner, had issued a call for refrigerator trucks to carry the remains to a temporary morgue at a nearby Air Force Reserve base.  
周五下午晚些时候开始移除遗体。县法医 Wayne Tatalovich 已发出调用冷藏卡车将遗体运送到附近空军预备役基地的临时停尸房。  
Examination tables, overhead lights, and portable X-ray machines also arrived. The job of identifying those on board Flight 427 from the pieces of remains required a team of pathologists from local hospitals and odontologists from the Pennsylvania Dental Identification Team.  
检查桌、吊灯和便携式 X 射线机也到达了。从残骸中识别 427 航班上人员的工作需要来自当地医院的病理学家和宾夕法尼亚牙科鉴定队的牙科医生组成的小组。  
From Mercyhurst College, in Erie, a forensic anthropologist, whose normal subjects were prehistoric human beings and their skeletal remains, volunteered his services.  
来自伊利市的 Mercyhurst College，一位法医人类学家，他的研究对象是史前人类及其骨骼遗骸，自愿提供他的服务。  
And, at Tatalovich’s request, a team of F.B.I. fingerprint specialists were scheduled to arrive on Saturday morning.  
在塔塔洛维奇的要求下，一支 F.B.I.指纹专家团队计划于周六上午到达。  
Nonetheless, having surveyed the carnage at the crash site, Tatalovich told reporters he doubted whether more than twenty or twenty-five per cent of the victims would be positively identified.  
尽管如此，在调查了事故现场的大屠杀后，塔塔洛维奇告诉记者，他怀疑超过 20%或 25%的受害者能否被确认为身份。

Back at the command post that afternoon, Haueter was on the phone to the Safety Board lab in Washington. Investigators there had pried open the flight-data recorder, and found it in even worse condition than the cockpit voice recorder.  
那天下午回到指挥中心，豪特尔正在给华盛顿安全委员会实验室打电话。那里的调查人员已经撬开了飞行数据记录仪，发现它的状况比驾驶舱语音记录仪还要糟糕。  
Inside, they discovered dirt and pieces of insulation. The memory module had separated from its mounts and crashed through all the circuit boards. But the module itself was intact.  
内部，他们发现灰尘和绝缘材料碎片。内存模块已从其支架上分离并穿过所有电路板。但模块本身完好无损。  
The investigators connected it to a working flight-data recorder, kept in the lab especially for this purpose, and downloaded all the data.  
研究人员将其连接到实验室为该目的特别保留的工作飞行数据记录仪，并下载了所有数据。

As Haueter had feared, Flight 427’s recorder was one of the more primitive varieties. It had monitored only eleven basic flight parameters. It did not directly record the positions of flight-control surfaces—the ailerons, spoilers, leading-edge slats, and rudder.  
正如 Haueter 所担心的一样，427 航班记录仪是较为原始的类型之一。它只监控了 11 个基本飞行参数。它没有直接记录飞行控制面的位置——副翼、扰流板、前缘襟翼和方向舵。  
But it did provide some of the first clues about what had happened to Flight 427.  
但它确实提供了一些关于 427 航班发生了什么的最初线索。

The most striking thing that Haueter and his Safety Board colleagues noticed was a one-second burst in airspeed, from around a hundred and ninety knots to above a hundred and ninety-five knots, and then, a second later, back below a hundred and ninety knots.  
最引人注目的是，豪特和他的安全委员会同事们注意到，空速在一秒内从大约一百九十节飙升到超过一百九十五节，然后，一秒后，又回到了一百九十节以下。  
This had occurred twenty-six seconds before the moment of impact.  
这发生在撞击前 26 秒。  
Since it is physically impossible for an object the size and mass of a Boeing 737 to accelerate and decelerate in such a fashion, the investigators considered the likelihood that the speed spike was simply a bad data point.  
由于一个大小和重量与波音 737 相当的对象以这种方式加速和减速在物理上是不可能的，调查人员认为速度峰值只是一个错误的数据点。  
But the spike was accompanied by small, rapid fluctuations in vertical and longitudinal acceleration, which the passengers on board would have felt as mild g forces. All in all, it bore the classic signs of an encounter with turbulence.  
但是，这次峰值伴随着垂直和纵向加速度的小幅快速波动，乘客会感觉到轻微的 g 力。总的来说，它具有遭遇湍流的典型特征。  
The speed spike, the investigators theorized, might reflect a change in air pressure which the sensors had momentarily read as a burst of speed.  
研究人员推测，速度激增可能反映了气压的变化，传感器短暂地将其读作速度爆发。

The flight-data recorder also showed that the plane’s heading had changed by two degrees, nose left, three seconds after the speed spike, and, a second later, an abrupt change of almost six degrees to the left.  
飞行数据记录仪还显示，在速度峰值后的三秒钟内，飞机航向改变了两个度，机头向左，紧接着一秒后，航向突然向左改变近六个度。  
The heading changes suggested that the plane had experienced a yawing moment—that the tail section had swung to the right while the nose moved left. It had, in essence, skidded in the air.  
标题更改表明飞机经历了偏航力矩——即尾部向右摆动，而机头向左移动。本质上，它在空中打滑。

Yaws are almost always induced by a movement of the rudder, the large, hinged vertical panel in the tail.  
舵叶几乎总是由舵的移动引起的，舵是尾部的那个大型的铰接式垂直面板。  
Like the rudder of a ship, the rudder of an airplane can be used to change the plane’s direction, but in practice pilots on commercial jet transports rarely use it for that purpose.  
像船的舵一样，飞机的舵可以用来改变飞机的方向，但在实际操作中，商用喷气式客机的飞行员很少用它来达到这个目的。  
Yaws discommode passengers: they create lateral acceleration—especially toward the rear of a plane—that can knock passengers off their feet and break bones.  
Yaws 会使乘客感到不适：它们会产生横向加速度——尤其是在飞机的后部——这可能会使乘客失去平衡并折断骨头。  
To change headings, commercial pilots use the ailerons, small horizontal hinged panels at the back edge of each wing, to enter gentle banks.  
要改变航向，商用飞行员使用副翼，即位于每个机翼后缘的小型水平铰接面板，进行柔和的转弯。  
In fact, virtually the only times that commercial pilots use the rudder are in the event of an engine failure on takeoff or when landing in a crosswind, and then the rudder is used to correct a yaw, not to induce it.  
实际上，商业飞行员几乎只在起飞时发动机故障或降落逆风时使用方向舵，此时使用方向舵是为了纠正偏航，而不是引起偏航。

But, according to the data recorder, both engines on Flight 427 were functioning normally. Reading down the columns of numbers, the investigators saw that the yawing moment was followed by a sharply increased roll to the left.  
但是，根据数据记录仪，427 航班上的两个引擎都正常工作。研究人员阅读数字列时，看到偏航力矩之后紧接着是向左急剧增加的滚转。  
As the plane’s nose pivoted left, the right wing would have moved more swiftly through the air, gaining lift and forcing the left wing down. In the span of a mere eight seconds, the left wing heeled over almost sixty degrees.  
随着飞机的机头向左转动，右翼会更快地穿过空气，获得升力并迫使左翼下降。在短短八秒内，左翼几乎倾斜了六十度。  
Seconds later, the bank angle reached a hundred and thirty-eight degrees to the left and the pitch eighty-six degrees nose down—nearly vertical—and by then the crash was inevitable.  
几秒钟后，飞机的银行角达到左一百三十八度，俯仰角八十六度——几乎垂直——那时，坠毁已不可避免。

Any pilot, especially one of Emmett’s experience, should have reacted swiftly and instinctively to a yaw and ensuing roll. He would have turned the steering column to the right, using the ailerons to bring the plane back to level flight. This was not a complicated maneuver.  
任何飞行员，尤其是像埃米特那样有经验的飞行员，都应该迅速本能地对偏航和随之而来的滚转做出反应。他会将方向盘转向右侧，使用副翼将飞机恢复到水平飞行。这并不是一个复杂的机动动作。  
Even a novice pilot in a Piper Cub would have the skill and the sense to do it.  
即使是 Piper Cub 的初级飞行员也能具备完成它的技能和意识。

But Haueter could not know what Emmett had done. A more advanced flight-data recorder, one that monitored the position of the flight-control surfaces, would have told him. It might also have revealed the cause of the yaw, but that information was lost to Haueter forever.  
但豪特无法知道埃米特做了什么。一个更先进的飞行数据记录器，一个可以监控飞行控制面位置的记录器，本可以告诉他。它也可能揭示了偏航的原因，但这个信息永远丢失了，对豪特来说也是如此。  
He and the other investigators would have to piece together by deduction and inference what had happened in those initial seconds of Flight 427’s upset.  
他和其他调查人员必须通过演绎和推理拼凑出 427 航班失事最初几秒钟内发生了什么。

III—Causes du Jour  III—今日原因
----------------------------

That Friday night, at the first progress meeting, Haueter went around the crowded room, person by person, asking, “Who are you and what group are you on?”  
那个周五晚上，在第一次进度会议上，豪伊特绕着拥挤的房间逐个询问：“你是谁，你属于哪个小组？”

“I’m from USAir, and I’m here to observe,” said a vice-president of USAir.  
我是 USAir 的，我来这里是为了观察，”USAir 的一位副总裁说。

“Please leave,” said Haueter politely.  
请离开，”豪特尔礼貌地说。

“This is important. I’m here to represent the company.”  
这是重要的。我在这儿代表公司。

“If you’re not participating in the investigation, please leave.”  
“如果您不参与调查，请离开。”

“I’m not going,” said the executive.  
我不去，”那位高管说。

“Yes, you are,” said Haueter.  
“是的，你就是，”豪特说。

“Our company rules require that a senior—”  
我们的公司规定，高级——

“No,” said Haueter. “Your company rules are in conflict with my rules. Please leave right now.”  
“不，”豪特说。“你们公司的规定与我的规定相冲突。请立刻离开。”

Haueter went through this process every night for the first five days. It took him half an hour. At times, his stomach rebelled at the confrontation. “People got angry, and it burned up a lot of time, but it was necessary,” he recalled.  
豪特每天晚上都要经历这个过程前五天。这花了他半小时。有时，他的胃会反抗这种对抗。“人们会生气，这浪费了很多时间，但这是必要的，”他回忆道。  
“When an accident is so big and controversial, it seems that you’re on the verge of losing control at any minute.”  
当一起事故如此重大且具有争议性时，似乎你随时都有失去控制的风险。

Haueter eventually pared attendance at the progress meetings down to a hundred people. Among the ten Safety Board groups assembled at Pittsburgh, there were around ninety investigators, nearly all of whom Haueter came to know by sight, if not by name.  
Haueter 最终将进度会议的出席人数减少到一百人。在匹兹堡组建的十个安全委员会小组中，大约有九十名调查员，Haueter 如果不是通过名字，也至少通过面容认识他们中的大多数。

Only about fifty investigators, on average, worked at the crash site itself. On Saturday morning, in the woods, an investigator found a small hydraulic device—one of six actuators on each engine that deploy the thrust reverser.  
平均只有大约五十名调查员在事故现场工作。周六早上，在树林里，一名调查员发现了一个小型液压装置——每个发动机上的六个执行器之一，用于部署推力反向器。  
The actuator, from the right engine, appeared open, in the extended position. This seemed highly significant. Thrust reversers are used only to slow a plane on landing, after it has touched down. A midair deployment would cause a catastrophic loss of control.  
执行器从右侧发动机处看起来是开启的，处于展开位置。这似乎非常关键。推力反转器仅在飞机着陆后用于减速，在飞机接触地面后。空中部署将导致灾难性的失控。  
One such deployment had occurred in 1991, on a Boeing 767 departing Bangkok. Everyone on board—two hundred and twenty-three people—had died in that crash.  
1991 年，在从曼谷起飞的一架波音 767 上发生了一起这样的部署。飞机上的所有人——223 人——在那次坠机中丧生。

On Sunday, the power-plant group discovered two more right-engine actuators that seemed to be in the deployed position. By contrast, five of the six actuators for the left engine—one was still missing—had been found in the stowed position.  
周日，发电厂小组发现了两个似乎处于展开位置的右引擎执行器。相比之下，六个左引擎执行器中的五个——还有一个缺失——被发现处于存放位置。  
The evidence to support a midair deployment of the thrust reverser seemed compelling, but it was also baffling. If the right engine had suddenly reversed thrust, it would have caused the plane’s nose to pivot violently to the right.  
支持在空中投放推力反向器的证据似乎很有说服力，但它也令人困惑。如果右引擎突然反向推力，飞机的机头会猛地向右偏转。  
Yet the flight-data recorder showed that the plane had rolled hard left.  
然而，飞行数据记录仪显示飞机向左滚转得很厉害。  
Trying to fit the facts to the theory, some investigators speculated that a partial deployment—deployment of one of the two sleeves—might have created a bizarre aerodynamic condition sufficient to roll the plane left.  
试图将事实与理论相符，一些调查人员推测，部分部署——部署其中一只袖子——可能产生足以使飞机向左翻滚的奇特空气动力学条件。  
This hypothesis met with skepticism in some quarters, and, late at night, as the parties gathered behind closed doors in their hotel rooms, even with ridicule.  
这一假设在一些地方遭到了怀疑，而且，深夜时分，当人们在酒店房间里关上门聚在一起时，甚至遭到了嘲笑。  
As for Haueter, he wasn’t interested in theories as much as in facts, and the fact of the open actuators could be neither denied nor explained.  
至于 Haueter，他更感兴趣的是事实而非理论，开放式执行器的这一事实既不能否认也不能解释。

In the days that followed, one interesting fact after another emerged, along with theories to accommodate them. Each theory, in turn, became known among the investigators as the cause du jour.  
在随后的日子里，一个又一个有趣的事实相继出现，随之而来的是一些理论来适应它们。每个理论，依次在调查者中成为当天的原因。  
The power-plant group, for example, reported on Sunday that after an exhaustive search it could not find the rear mount that secured the right engine to the wing pylon. The engine-mount theory became Monday’s _cause du jour_. Unlike the thrust-reverser theory, a failure of the right-rear engine mount, resulting in the engine’s tilting upward, might have rolled the plane to the left.  
电厂小组例如，在周日报告称，经过彻底搜索，他们找不到固定右引擎到机翼支柱的后部支架。引擎支架理论成为周一的热门话题。与反推力理论不同，右后引擎支架的故障，导致引擎向上倾斜，可能使飞机向左翻滚。

The engine mount finally did turn up, in the USAir hangar, much to the embarrassment of the power-plant group. It had been found on Friday and removed from the site, but someone had failed to log it in. Haueter quietly fumed.  
发动机支架终于出现了，在美国航空机库中，这让动力装置小组感到非常尴尬。它是在周五被发现的，并从现场移走，但有人未能记录下来。豪特尔默默地生气。  
But by then suspicious fractures in the actuator of the No. 1 leading-edge slat had become the new _cause du jour_. And the thrust-reverser conundrum was solved when investigators dismantled those actuators and found they had not, in fact, been deployed; the rods connected to the pistons had broken off on impact, so the actuators only looked as if they were extended.  
但那时，1 号前缘襟翼执行器中的可疑裂缝已成为新的当务之急。当调查人员拆解这些执行器时，他们发现实际上并没有部署；连接到活塞的杆在撞击时断裂，所以执行器看起来像是被伸展开的。

Within three days, Haueter and the assembled investigators had learned a great deal about the circumstances of the accident but nothing to suggest a probable cause.  
在三天内，豪特和聚集的调查人员对事故的情况了解了很多，但没有任何迹象表明可能的原因。  
The meteorology group reported that the day of the event had been calm, with no weather advisories and no discernible bird activity, and other pilots had reported no turbulence.  
气象小组报告称，活动当天天气平静，没有发布任何天气预警，也没有观察到明显的鸟类活动，其他飞行员也没有报告到湍流。  
The operations group, investigating the personal histories and careers of Emmett and Germano, found unblemished records—no disciplinary actions, no evidence of unusual medical problems. The human-performance group, looking into the pilots’ last seventy-two hours, found no indication of erratic behavior, or drug or alcohol use.  
操作组调查了埃米特和德国诺的个人历史和职业生涯，发现记录无瑕疵——没有纪律处分，没有异常医疗问题的证据。人类性能组调查了飞行员最后的七十二小时，没有发现异常行为或药物或酒精使用的迹象。  
They had not yet succeeded, however, in finding the pilots’ remains, for alcohol and drug testing—standard in all Safety Board accident investigations. Haueter pressed them to continue their search.  
然而，他们还没有找到飞行员遗体，因为要进行酒精和药物检测——这是所有安全委员会事故调查的标准程序。豪特尔敦促他们继续搜寻。

After the Sunday progress meeting, Ed Kittel, the bomb expert, called Calvin Walbert, his colleague at the F.A.A. Aviation Explosives Security Unit. He told Walbert that he’d uncovered no evidence of an explosion yet, but that the investigation was still wide open.  
周日进度会议之后，炸弹专家埃德·基特尔给他在联邦航空管理局航空爆炸物安全单位的同事卡尔文·沃尔伯特打电话。他告诉沃尔伯特，他还没有发现爆炸的证据，但调查仍然全面展开。  
Since Kittel was occupied full time at the crash site examining wreckage, he needed Walbert’s help at the morgue.  
自克特尔全天在事故现场检查残骸，他需要在停尸房时得到瓦尔伯特的帮助。  
Standard procedure in a suspected bomb investigation calls for X-ray examination of all human remains, on the theory that bomb components or shrapnel bearing an explosive signature might be embedded in the victims’ flesh.  
疑似炸弹调查的标准程序要求对所有人体遗骸进行 X 光检查，依据是炸弹组件或带有爆炸特征的弹片可能嵌入受害者的肉体内。

Walbert arrived in Pittsburgh on Monday and took his station at the morgue. A pathologist removed foreign particles embedded in the body parts, and Walbert scrutinized each artifact in turn.  
沃尔伯特于周一抵达匹兹堡，并在太平间就位。病理学家从身体部位中移除了异物，沃尔伯特依次仔细检查了每一件物品。  
By the time he finished his grisly task, eight days later, he had many buckets of metal and plastic debris, but none of it pointed to a bomb exploding aboard Flight 427.  
在他完成那项令人毛骨悚然的任务后，八天过去了，他收集了许多桶金属和塑料碎片，但没有任何迹象表明 427 航班上有炸弹爆炸。

Up at the crash site, Kittel found no evidence of a bomb, either. On Thursday, with all but the smallest fragments removed from the site, he departed for the USAir hangar, where the structures group had begun laying out the wreckage in a rough approximation of the airplane.  
在事故现场，基特尔也没有找到炸弹的证据。周四，随着现场除最微小的碎片外都被移除，他前往美国航空机库，那里结构组已经开始将残骸大致摆放成飞机的样子。  
“By then I was feeling pretty confident it was not an explosion,” Kittel recalled. “I would have been surprised if I’d found something in the hangar that I’d missed in the field.”  
那时我觉得这肯定不是一次爆炸，”基特尔回忆道。“如果在机库中发现我在现场错过的东西，我会感到惊讶。”

Not far from Kittel’s decontamination station, two doctors, one from the F.A.A.’s Civil Aeromedical Institute and the other a USAir flight surgeon, searched for the remains of Germano and Emmett.  
不远处的 Kittel 去污站，两名医生，一名来自美国联邦航空管理局的民用航空医学研究所，另一名是 USAir 的飞行外科医生，正在寻找 Germano 和 Emmett 的遗体。  
They concentrated their efforts, quite logically, on the grid where the greatest number of cockpit fragments were found.  
他们集中精力，相当合理地，在发现最多驾驶舱碎片的地方进行搜索。  
Their work commanded the interest of all the parties to the investigation, for there was a lot at stake—potentially hundreds of millions of dollars in liability—in ascertaining whether the body parts presumed to belong to Emmett and Germano were free of alcohol and drugs.  
他们的工作引起了所有参与调查方的兴趣，因为这里涉及的利益很大——可能涉及数亿美元的责任——以确定被认为是埃米特和德国诺的遗体部分是否不含酒精和毒品。  
The rear of the plane, after all, had met the front with explosive force within a fraction of a second of impact: bodies and seats had hurtled forward, and some of those bodies had just consumed alcohol served by the USAir flight attendants.  
飞机的后部，毕竟在撞击的一瞬间就与前方以爆炸般的力相遇：身体和座椅向前飞去，其中一些身体刚刚喝下了美国航空乘务员提供的酒精饮料。

The doctors relied heavily on circumstantial evidence, looking for flesh and bone in proximity to the cockpit and to scraps of USAir uniform and other personal effects.  
医生们严重依赖间接证据，寻找驾驶舱附近的肉体和骨骼，以及美国航空制服碎片和其他个人物品。  
Under better circumstances, they might have hoped to find the pilots’ heads, and used the teeth to provide positive identification and the vitreous of the eyeball, which can resist deterioration up to three days, for toxicological tests.  
在更好的情况下，他们可能希望找到飞行员的头部，并使用牙齿进行正面识别，以及眼球玻璃体，它可以抵抗恶化长达三天，用于毒理学测试。  
These circumstances, however, were far from ideal. There were no intact heads, never mind organs as fragile as eyeballs.  
这些情况，然而，远非理想。没有完好的头部，更不用说像眼球一样脆弱的器官了。

The doctors sorted through countless human remains, looking for leads—for part of a hand, for example, bearing a wedding band, or some other personal effect a family member would recognize.  
医生们翻检了无数的人类遗体，寻找线索——比如寻找带着婚戒的手的一部分，或者一些家庭成员能认出的其他个人物品。  
By late in the week, the morgue had singled out several items, including part of an upper left arm and sections of muscle from the lower-lumbar, thoracic, and cervical vertebrae. Pathologists shipped these remains to the Armed Forces DNA Identification Laboratory, in Maryland.  
本周晚些时候，法医已挑选出几件物品，包括左上臂的一部分和腰部、胸部和颈椎的肌肉部分。病理学家将这些遗体运送到位于马里兰州的武装部队 DNA 鉴定实验室。

DNA analysis confirmed that the specimens came from two distinct individuals, but positive identification of the pilots awaited DNA samples from their nearest relatives. Emmett’s mother contributed a blood sample.  
DNA 分析确认，这些样本来自两个不同的人，但需要从飞行员最近的亲属那里获取 DNA 样本才能进行正确定性。埃米特的母亲提供了一份血样。  
Some of the remains presumed to be Emmett’s proved, in fact, to be his. Germano’s relatives declined to provide specimens. Haueter says he does not know why.  
一些被认为是埃米特遗骸的物品实际上确实是他的。格曼诺的亲属拒绝提供样本。豪特说，他不知道为什么。

And there matters might have rested, with Germano unidentified, were it not for a fortuitous discovery.  
若非一次偶然的发现，事情可能就这样结束了，德国人身份不明。  
The investigators had also found, in the same general area as the presumed remains, the thin sole of a foot that had been pared off, in the words of one investigator, as if with a cheese slicer.  
调查人员在疑似遗骸所在的同一区域还发现了一块被削薄的脚底，一位调查人员用这样的话来形容：“就像用奶酪切片器削过一样。”  
As it happened, Germano had served in the Air Force, the only branch of the service that routinely footprints its air-crew members. The F.B.I. printed the sole, and it matched Germano’s footprint.  
德国诺曾在美国空军服役，这是唯一一支定期记录其飞行员足迹的部队。联邦调查局打印出了足迹，与德国诺的足迹相符。  
The sole, inadequate for toxicological testing, provided a DNA sample that confirmed the identity of Germano’s other body parts.  
该单一样本，不足以进行毒理学测试，提供了一份 DNA 样本，确认了 Germano 其他身体部位的身份。

In the end, the remains of both Germano and Emmett tested free of drugs but positive for small amounts of ethanol. This, the investigators agreed, was almost certainly the product of putrefaction—of five days of exposure and decay at the crash site.  
最终，德国诺和埃米特的遗体检测均未发现药物，但乙醇含量呈阳性。调查人员一致认为，这几乎肯定是腐败的结果——五天在事故现场的暴露和腐烂。

Haueter heard from Washington that a pilot, a member of the cockpit-voice-recorder team, had detected a sound on the tape—it was described as a “whoosh”—that the pilot thought might have come from the electronics-and-equipment-bay door blowing open.  
Haueter 从华盛顿得知，一名飞行员，该飞行员是驾驶舱语音记录器团队的一员，在磁带上检测到了一种声音——被描述为“呼啸声”——飞行员认为这可能是电子设备舱门打开时产生的声音。  
That door was just behind the nosewheel, and if it had blown open in flight the voice recorder might have captured just such a sound. Moreover, a door blowing open in flight might have played a role in the ensuing upset. Suddenly, the door became a _cause du jour_. Members of the structures group rummaged through dozens of bins of debris. They found mangled parts of the door, most significantly a four-by-nine-inch section with the latching mechanism and a piece of the frame attached. The pin was down, in the latched position.  
那扇门就在起落架后面，如果它在飞行中打开，话筒可能捕捉到了这样的声音。此外，飞行中门打开可能对随后的翻覆起到了作用。突然，这扇门成了当务之急。结构组成员翻遍了数十个垃圾箱。他们找到了门扭曲的部分，最重要的是一个四乘九英寸的带锁定机构的部分和一块框架。销钉处于锁定位置。  
Some weeks later, Haueter learned that further analysis of the voice recorder had determined that the “whoosh” might be Germano taking a sharp breath. “Once somebody raises it, you’ve got to chase it down,” Haueter remarked, with a shrug.  
几周后，豪特了解到对录音机的进一步分析确定，“呼啸”可能是德国诺呼吸急促。豪特耸了耸肩说：“一旦有人提出，你就必须追查到底。”

Finding all the doors and hatches—there are eleven on a 737—was a priority, as it is in every accident investigation. Only one eyewitness out of dozens interviewed reported seeing anything fall from the plane before it plummeted to earth, and this witness was a six-year-old boy.  
找到所有门和舱口——737 上有 11 个——是首要任务，这在每一次事故调查中都是如此。在数十名被采访的目击者中，只有一名目击者报告说在飞机坠地前看到有东西从飞机上掉落，而这个目击者是一个六岁的男孩。  
But other reports had come in from residents who’d found small pieces of insulation and a business card belonging to one of the passengers more than two miles from the crash site. This suggested the possibility of a door having come open.  
但其他报告显示，居民们发现了一些绝缘材料的小碎片和一张乘客的名片，这些物品距离坠机现场超过两英里。这表明可能有一扇门打开了。

Haueter sent helicopters to conduct an aerial search of the last fifteen miles of the plane’s flight path. The searchers spotted a large blue-and-white object high in a tree. It turned out to be a kite.  
豪特尔派直升机对飞机飞行路径的最后十五英里进行空中搜索。搜救人员发现了一个高挂在树上的大蓝白色物体。结果证明那是一个风筝。  
On Wednesday, the sixth day of the investigation, a hundred and forty volunteers—pilots, mechanics, and ordinary people—and twenty rescue workers took to the ground. They found nothing from the plane.  
周三，调查的第六天，一百四十名志愿者——飞行员、机械师和普通人——以及二十名救援人员来到现场。他们从飞机上什么也没找到。

Haueter made a point of visiting the crash site twice a day, but his real work took place at the command post. Several of the investigative groups never went to the crash site.  
豪特每天两次去事故现场，但他的真正工作是在指挥所进行的。几个调查小组从未去过事故现场。  
Some members of the aircraft-performance group, for example, spent their time in air-conditioned offices, parsing the flight-data recorder’s raw numbers and matching them with the radar tracking plot.  
一些飞机性能小组成员，例如，他们在空调办公室里度过时间，解析飞行数据记录仪的原始数字，并将它们与雷达跟踪图匹配。

They made several important discoveries. Working with the radar track, they found that USAir 427 had intersected the flight path of the preceding Delta 727 at virtually the precise moment of the yaw.  
他们做出了几个重要发现。在与雷达轨迹一起工作时，他们发现 USAir 427 几乎在偏航的精确时刻与先前的 Delta 727 的飞行路径相交。  
The Delta, they calculated, was at that time 4.2 miles ahead of Flight 427—about a minute and ten seconds—and three hundred feet higher. F.A.A. regulations call for a minimum lateral separation of three miles, and the planes had kept well outside that limit.  
Delta，他们计算，当时在 427 航班前方 4.2 英里——大约一分零十秒——并且高出 300 英尺。联邦航空管理局的规定要求最小横向距离为 3 英里，而飞机保持在那个限制之外。  
But it appeared probable that Flight 427 had encountered the Delta’s wake vortex, which, given the calmness of the day, would have descended largely intact. Moreover, it provided a possible explanation for the turbulence that had resulted in the phantom speed spike.  
但似乎很可能，427 航班遭遇了达美航空的尾流涡旋，鉴于当天的平静，它将基本完好地下降。此外，这也为导致幽灵速度激增的湍流提供了一个可能的解释。

Two things, then, appeared to have come together at a moment in time—a wake-vortex encounter and the yaw. Yet it seemed preposterous to the investigators that a wake vortex could induce a yaw that would flip a plane the size of a 737 into a fatal roll.  
两件事似乎在某个时刻同时发生——尾流涡旋遭遇和偏航。然而，调查人员认为，尾流涡旋不可能引起足以使一架 737 大小的飞机翻转成致命滚转的偏航。  
Pilots routinely encounter wake vortices, especially near busy airports. “It probably occurs four times an hour at National Airport,” Haueter said. “If it’s a problem, why isn’t the ground littered with 737s falling out of the sky?”  
飞行员经常遇到尾流涡旋，尤其是在繁忙的机场附近。“在国家机场，这可能每小时发生四次，”Haueter 说。“如果这是个问题，为什么地面上没有散落着从天而降的 737 飞机？”

Nonetheless, he accepted the vortex as a fact, another in a large mosaic of facts.  
尽管如此，他将涡旋视为事实，这是众多事实中的一个，构成了一个庞大的事实拼图。

Greg Phillips, meanwhile, had spent much of the week up at the crash site and at the USAir hangar, where he supervised the work of his group, aircraft systems. He and Haueter did not see much of each other, except at the evening progress meetings and again, briefly, in the mornings, when Haueter met for twenty minutes with the Safety Board group leaders.  
格雷格·菲利普斯，与此同时，这周的大部分时间都在事故现场和美国航空机库，在那里他监督他团队的工作，即飞机系统。他和豪特之间很少见面，除了晚上的进度会议，以及在早上，当豪特与安全委员会小组领导会面二十分钟时。  
“I don’t have to give Greg a lot of direction,” Haueter said. “He’s capable of running without my guidance. I wish I could clone him and have him do several things at once.”  
我不需要给格雷格太多指导，”豪特说。“他可以在没有我的指导下独立行动。我希望我能复制他，让他一次做几件事。”

Until the moment Phillips first saw the printout of the flight-data recorder, he’d given no thought to Colorado Springs, the accident he’d worked on to no avail for almost two years.  
直到菲利普斯第一次看到飞行数据记录器的打印输出，他从未想过科罗拉多斯普林斯，他在那里努力了将近两年，但事故仍未得到解决。  
But the flight data and the tracking plots now drawn up by the performance group bore a distinct similarity to what had happened at Colorado Springs.  
但是性能小组现在绘制的飞行数据和跟踪图与在科罗拉多斯普林斯发生的情况有明显的相似之处。  
Both 737s had experienced a catastrophic loss of control on approach—a yaw followed by a violent roll, loss of lift, and an almost vertical plunge.  
两架 737 在接近时都经历了灾难性的失控——先是偏航，随后是剧烈的滚转，升力丧失，几乎是垂直下坠。

There were differences, of course. Colorado Springs had happened at one thousand feet, not six thousand; the plane had rolled right, not left; and the yaw had not been nearly as severe. Turbulence had become a major issue in that investigation.  
当然有差异。科罗拉多斯普林斯事件发生在 1000 英尺处，而不是 6000 英尺；飞机是向右滚转，而不是向左；偏航也没有那么严重。湍流成为了那次调查的主要问题。  
Mountain wind rotors—horizontal funnels of air coming off the Rockies—had been reported on the day of the crash.  
山风涡轮——来自落基山的水平空气旋涡——在坠机当天已被报道。  
But other, lighter planes had landed without incident that day, and, moreover, a mountain rotor had never been known to knock a plane the size of a 737 out of the sky.  
但那天其他、更轻的飞机已经安全着陆，而且，更重要的是，据所知，山旋翼机从未将一架 737 大小的飞机从空中击落。

The Colorado Springs investigation ultimately came down to an examination of the plane’s rudder system. It was up to Phillips to determine whether the power-control units that drove the rudder had malfunctioned. That plane had a history of rudder “bumps,” as they are called.  
科罗拉多斯普林斯调查最终归结于对飞机方向舵系统的检查。由菲利普斯负责确定驱动方向舵的电力控制单元是否出现故障。那架飞机有方向舵“颠簸”的历史。  
In the week before the crash, two different flight crews had reported uncommanded rudder movements resulting in yaws, both to the right, the direction of the fatal roll.  
在坠机前的一周，两个不同的机组成员报告了未经授权的舵面移动，导致偏航，都是向右，这是致命滚转的方向。  
On both occasions, mechanics had made repairs to the yaw damper, a device that during flight makes fine adjustments to the rudder position.  
在两次事件中，机械师都对偏航阻尼器进行了维修，这是一种在飞行中对舵位进行微调的装置。  
Swept-wing aircraft require yaw dampers because of an aerodynamic phenomenon known as Dutch roll—a tendency to oscillate back and forth, as someone on ice skates might.  
翼型飞机需要偏航阻尼器，因为存在一种称为荷兰滚转的空气动力学现象——就像滑冰者来回摆动一样。  
The yaw damper, which, by design, can move the rudder only a few degrees in either direction, dampens these oscillations automatically, without constant input by the pilot.  
偏航阻尼器，按设计只能将舵只在两个方向上移动几度，能自动抑制这些振荡，无需飞行员持续输入。

Yaw dampers have proved over the years to be one of the most troublesome components of the 737’s flight-control systems. But, because of their built-in limitation, investigators don’t believe they pose a threat to flight safety.  
多年来，阻尼器已被证明是 737 飞行控制系统中最麻烦的部件之一。但是，由于它们内置的限制，调查人员不相信它们对飞行安全构成威胁。  
Confronted with an erratic yaw damper, many pilots have been trained, and others have simply learned, to switch the system off.  
面对不稳定的偏航阻尼器，许多飞行员都接受了培训，而其他人则简单地学会了关闭系统。

All the same, there’s no doubt in Phillips’s mind that the yaw dampers on the Boeing 737 could be made more reliable. And it was obviously significant that yaw-damper troubles had preceded the Colorado Springs crash.  
尽管如此，菲利普斯心中无疑认为波音 737 的偏航阻尼器可以做得更可靠。而且，偏航阻尼器问题发生在科罗拉多斯普林斯空难之前显然具有重要意义。  
Throughout the twenty-one-month investigation, Phillips examined and tested those fire-damaged rudder systems, including components salvaged from the yaw damper.  
在整个 21 个月的调查中，菲利普斯检查并测试了那些火灾损坏的舵系统，包括从偏航阻尼器中抢救出的部件。  
He found plenty that was wrong—wear and galling on the standby rudder power-control unit, for example—but in the end he did not find a malfunction that would have caused the crash.  
他发现了许多问题——例如备用舵的电力控制单元磨损和磨损——但最终他没有发现会导致坠毁的故障。

That investigation closed without a finding of probable cause. The final report listed a number of possibilities, including the rudder system and an unusual atmospheric event. In the minds of some of the investigators, the rudder system remains the most likely culprit.  
该调查未发现可能的原因而结束。最终报告列出了几种可能性，包括舵系统和异常大气事件。在一些调查者的心中，舵系统仍然是可能性最大的罪魁祸首。  
Boeing, however, believes differently. It has stated that a mountain rotor probably caused the Colorado Springs crash.  
波音公司持不同意见。它表示，科罗拉多斯普林斯空难可能是由于山岳旋翼引起的。  
Boeing’s opinion notwithstanding, the Colorado Springs investigation and a subsequent event—the freezing up of a rudder on a United 737 just before takeoff at O’Hare Airport in 1992—resulted in the F.A.A. ordering improvements in the rudder power-control units on all Boeing 737s by 1999.  
尽管波音公司持有不同意见，但科罗拉多斯普林斯调查以及随后发生的一件事——1992 年，在奥黑尔机场起飞前，一架联合航空 737 的尾翼结冰——导致美国联邦航空管理局（F.A.A.）在 1999 年前要求对所有波音 737 的尾翼动力控制单元进行改进。  
In the interim, the F.A.A. requires airlines to test the units every seven hundred and fifty flight hours for signs of a jam or leakage of hydraulic fluid.  
在此期间，美国联邦航空管理局（F.A.A.）要求航空公司每飞行七百五十小时对设备进行测试，以检查是否存在堵塞或液压油泄漏的迹象。

With memories of the Colorado Springs investigation fresh in his mind, Phillips treated USAir 427’s rudder system with elaborate care.  
在科罗拉多斯普林斯调查的记忆仍然清晰，菲利普斯对 USAir 427 的舵系统进行了细致的保养。  
At the crash site, he and his team opened access panels on the tail section and found, to their good fortune, the main and standby power-control units and the yaw-damper system largely intact, with hydraulic lines, torque tubes, and input rods still connected.  
在事故现场，他和他的团队打开了尾部段的访问面板，幸运的是，他们发现主备用电源控制单元和偏航阻尼系统基本完好，液压管线、扭矩管和输入杆仍然连接。  
Phillips took photographs and measurements before removing the entire tail section to the USAir hangar.  
菲利普斯在将整个尾部移至 USAir 机库之前，先拍照并测量。

In the hangar, the team cut through the tail’s aluminum skin and shimmed the main input crank, which responds to the pilot’s commands, with safety wire to preserve its exact position. They cut away the attachments and extracted the power-control units.  
在机库中，团队切开了机尾的铝制外壳，并用安全线固定了主输入曲柄，该曲柄响应飞行员的指令，以保持其精确位置。他们切除了连接件并提取了动力控制单元。  
The entire device, which weighs sixty-five pounds and costs about a hundred thousand dollars, consists of dual concentric servo valves—two pistonlike slides, one within the other—that move in concert, causing the rudder to move.  
整个设备重六十五磅，价值约十万美元，由双同心伺服阀组成——两个活塞状滑块，一个在另一个内部，它们协同运动，使舵移动。  
The valves are manufactured to extremely fine tolerances: the gap between the outer slide and the housing is two microns, one-fortieth the thickness a human hair, and the slides themselves move a distance less than the thickness of a dime.  
阀门制造精度极高：外滑块与外壳之间的间隙为两微米，相当于一根人类头发的四十分之一，而滑块本身移动的距离小于一枚一角硬币的厚度。  
Yet the power-control unit drives the rudder through its full arc, fifty-two degrees, from one stop to the other.  
然而，动力控制单元将舵叶通过其整个圆弧，52 度，从一侧停止到另一侧。

Phillips did not have the equipment to test a device so finely calibrated. Because he didn’t want to risk shipping it, he intended to carry the piece by hand to Boeing’s facilities outside Seattle as soon as his work in Pittsburgh drew to a close.  
菲利普斯没有设备来测试这样精细校准的设备。因为他不想冒险运输，所以他打算在匹兹堡的工作结束后，亲自将这件物品带到西雅图外的波音设施。

And it was quickly drawing to a close. By Friday, September 16th, eight days after the crash, all the groups had completed their on-site documentation of the wreckage.  
到周五，9 月 16 日，事故发生后的第八天，所有小组都已完成了对残骸的现场文档记录。  
Haueter accepted an offer from the U.S. Bureau of Mines to have ground-penetrating radar brought in to search for the last buried fragments of the plane. On Saturday, a team of dogs swept the site, searching for human remains overlooked by the recovery crews.  
豪特接受美国矿业局提供的一项提议，将地面雷达引入以寻找飞机最后被埋藏的碎片。周六，一支搜救犬队对该地点进行了清理，寻找被救援队伍忽视的人类遗骸。  
Wayne Tatalovich, the county coroner, said he now believed that the morgue crews would succeed in identifying sixty per cent of the passengers.  
威恩·塔塔洛维奇，县法医，表示他现在相信法医团队将成功识别 60%的乘客。

On Sunday, Haueter prepared to depart for Washington, D.C. Earlier in the week, he told a reporter at the hangar, “We have a lot of data. We have a lot of airplane. . . .  
周日，豪特准备前往华盛顿特区。本周早些时候，他在机库告诉一名记者：“我们有很多数据。我们有很多飞机……”  
We just need time now to go through all this.” At the last press conference, he admitted that, as the Pittsburgh _Post-Gazette_ put it, he had “no working theory at this point about what caused the plane to flip over and slam into the earth.”  
我们现在只需要时间来处理这一切。”在最后的新闻发布会上，他承认，正如《匹兹堡邮报》所说，他“目前还没有关于飞机翻滚坠地的原因的工作理论。”

IV—Get on with Life  IV—继续生活
----------------------------

In the months that followed, one question occupied Haueter and every other USAir 427 investigator: What had caused the yaw? The leading scenario, acknowledged by many, was a malfunction of the rudder in conjunction with a wake-vortex encounter.  
在随后的几个月里，一个疑问占据了 Haueter 和其他所有 USAir 427 调查员的心：是什么导致了偏航？许多人都认可的领先情景是舵故障与遭遇尾流涡旋的结合。  
But Haueter wanted no possibility left unexamined.  
但豪特不想留下任何未被检查的可能性。  
And the possibilities, once adduced, seemed virtually endless—structural deformation of the vertical stabilizer, a collapsed floor beam impinging on the rudder cables, an electrical short circuit, a tire explosion in the wheel well severing a cable.  
一旦列举出来，可能性似乎几乎是无限的——垂直稳定器的结构变形、地板梁坍塌撞击舵缆、电路短路、轮胎在轮舱内爆炸切断电缆。  
Accordingly, all the investigative groups remained operational.  
因此，所有调查组都保持运作状态。

The structures group returned three times that fall to the USAir hangar at the Pittsburgh airport, where the wreckage remained under Safety Board lock and key. In October, Haueter decided that the structures group would have to reconstruct the airplane.  
该结构组三次将残骸运回美国航空公司的机场机库，该机库位于匹兹堡机场，残骸在安全委员会的锁和钥匙下保存。十月份，豪特决定结构组必须重建这架飞机。  
The group obtained from Boeing full-size blueprints, and laid them out on the hangar floor. To assist, the Safety Board brought over from Britain two senior investigators from the Air Accidents Investigation Branch.  
该小组从波音获得了全尺寸蓝图，并将它们铺在机库地板上。为了协助，安全委员会从英国调来了航空事故调查局的两名高级调查员。  
One of them, David King, had coördinated the reconstruction of the Pan Am 747 that crashed at Lockerbie, Scotland, in 1988.  
其中之一，大卫·金，曾协调 1988 年在苏格兰洛克比坠毁的泛美 747 客机的重建工作。

The structures group, now numbering more than thirty, spent nearly three weeks at the hangar. Its members concentrated their efforts on the floor beams, the rudder cables, the forward pressure bulkhead, the wheel wells, and the auxiliary fuel tank.  
结构组，现在人数超过三十人，在机库中度过了近三周时间。成员们集中精力在地板梁、舵机电缆、前压力舱壁、车轮井和辅助油箱上。  
They did most of their work with micrometers, measuring the thickness of thousands of tiny fragments of debris and comparing it to Boeing specifications for given parts of the airplane. “An extremely daunting task,” David King later observed.  
他们大部分工作使用千分尺，测量数千小块碎片厚度，并将其与波音公司对飞机特定部件的规格进行比较。“这是一项极其艰巨的任务，”大卫·金后来评论道。  
Comparing it to Lockerbie, he said, “The challenge presented by Flight 427 was a much more severe one. . . . Lockerbie had broken up in the air and the pieces had fallen to the ground. This plane had been driven at much higher speed into the ground.”  
与洛克比相比，他说：“427 航班所面临的挑战要严重得多。洛克比是在空中解体，碎片掉落在地面上。这架飞机是以更高的速度撞向地面的。”

The operations group, meanwhile, had tracked down passengers from USAir’s flight to Chicago. They found Captain Jackson, who had flown in the cockpit jump seat behind Germano and Emmett, and the businessman who had complained about the strange noise from the cabin ceiling.  
该行动小组同时追踪到了从美国航空飞往芝加哥的乘客。他们找到了杰克逊上尉，他曾在德国诺和埃米特后面的驾驶舱跳座上飞行，以及那位抱怨机舱天花板奇怪噪音的商人。  
Neither could provide any significant information. To this day, the investigators have not found the woman who, in apparent concern for her husband’s safety, spoke with the USAir maintenance foreman in Chicago.  
既不能提供任何重要信息。时至今日，调查人员仍未找到那位出于对丈夫安全的担忧，在芝加哥与美国航空维修主管交谈的妇女。

At the cockpit-voice-recorder lab in Washington, Jim Cash invited pilots of 737s in to listen to the accident tape. Initially, none could come up with an explanation for the thumps or the clicks.  
在华盛顿的驾驶舱语音记录器实验室，吉姆·卡斯邀请 737 飞行员来听事故录音。最初，没有人能解释出这些砰砰声或点击声的原因。  
They could not recall hearing those particular sounds, although they could not be certain that they’d never heard them, either. In most respects, the sounds seemed unremarkable, perhaps caused by something as banal as a flight bag falling over or a pilot moving his foot.  
他们无法回忆起听到过那些特定的声音，尽管他们也无法确定自己从未听过。在大多数方面，这些声音似乎很平常，可能是由像飞行包掉落或飞行员移动脚这样的平凡事情引起的。  
It was not the sounds themselves so much as when they occurred that made them significant.  
它之所以重要，与其说是声音本身，不如说是它们出现的时候。

The performance group, attempting to replicate the sequence of numbers gleaned from USAir 427’s flight-data recorder, flew more than two hundred runs on Boeing’s flight simulator, testing all kinds of maneuvers and failure and malfunction scenarios.  
该性能小组试图复制从 USAir 427 航班数据记录仪中获取的数字序列，在波音的飞行模拟器上进行了两百多次飞行，测试了各种机动和故障及故障情况。  
They also constructed a kinematic model, using the eleven known parameters from the flight-data recorder and other pieces of information—such as the rate of the wind and the plane’s center of gravity—to arrive at the aerodynamic coefficients necessary to divine the position of the 737’s flight controls.  
他们还构建了一个运动学模型，使用飞行数据记录器中的 11 个已知参数以及其他信息——如风速和飞机的重心——来确定 737 飞行控制位置所需的空气动力学系数。  
Since they were dealing with many variables—the speed and position of the plane as well as the wake vortex—the possible permutations seemed almost infinite. No single scenario provided a perfect match.  
由于他们需要处理许多变量——飞机的速度和位置以及尾流涡旋——可能的排列组合似乎几乎是无限的。没有单一场景能提供完美匹配。  
Within the confines of the known parameters, they couldn’t seem to make the simulator do what they knew the plane had done.  
在已知参数的范围内，他们似乎无法让模拟器做到他们知道飞机已经做到的事情。

But they came very close. The best match occurred with the rudder jammed all the way over to the left, at between twenty and twenty-two degrees. By now, everyone agreed that for this accident to happen the rudder had to have moved. But what had caused it to move?  
但他们非常接近。最佳匹配发生在舵完全向左卡住，在二十到二十二度之间。现在，每个人都同意，要发生这次事故，舵必须移动。但究竟是什么原因导致它移动的呢？

Greg Phillips, with the power-control unit in his custody, attempted to determine whether it was the culprit. The systems group convened first at Boeing and then at the facilities of the Parker Hannifin Corporation, the manufacturer of the main power-control unit.  
格雷格·菲利普斯，在保管着电力控制单元的情况下，试图确定它是否是罪魁祸首。系统小组首先在波音公司，然后在主电力控制单元制造商帕克·汉宁菲恩公司的设施中召开会议。  
At the Parker labs, in Irvine, California, the investigators drained the hydraulic fluid, which they saved for analysis of contamination, and disassembled the unit. Under microscopes, they examined the valves and their housing for evidence of abnormal wear, binding, or galling.  
在加州欧文市的帕克实验室，调查人员排出了液压油，将其保存以供污染分析，并拆解了该装置。在显微镜下，他们检查了阀门及其外壳，寻找异常磨损、卡住或磨损的证据。  
Back at Boeing, they had found a small amount of galling on the standby rudder unit (which was not made by Parker), but they found nothing on the main. They reassembled the unit, mounted it on a test stand, and ran it repeatedly, driving it to the limit of its stops.  
在波音公司，他们在备用舵机（不是帕克公司制造的）上发现了一小部分磨损，但在主舵机上没有发现任何问题。他们重新组装了该单元，将其安装在测试台上，并反复运行，直到达到其停止极限。  
It functioned normally.  它运行正常。

The new chairman of the Safety Board, James Hall, convened a public hearing into the crash of USAir 427 in late January, 1995, four and a half months after the accident.  
新任安全委员会主席詹姆斯·霍尔于 1995 年 1 月下旬，即事故四个月后，召开了关于 USAir 427 航班坠机事件的公开听证会。  
He opened the hearing by admitting that it was “no secret that the aviation community is concerned about this accident . . . because this is the second accident in nearly four years involving a Boeing 737 for which as yet no cause has been readily identified.”  
他通过承认“航空界对这起事故的关注并非秘密……因为这是近四年来第二起涉及波音 737 的事故，至今尚未找到直接原因。”来开启了听证会。

The hearing lasted five days. Thirty-one witnesses testified about all areas of the investigation. One of them was David King, who had assisted the structures group in its reconstruction efforts. King told the following story.  
听证会持续了五天。三十一名证人就调查的所有领域作证。其中一人是大卫·金，他曾协助结构组进行重建工作。金讲述了以下故事。

On October 7, 1993, a British Airways Boeing 747 with three hundred and eighty-nine passengers on board took off from London’s Heathrow Airport en route to Bangkok. It followed by only two minutes the departure of another fully laden 747.  
1993 年 10 月 7 日，一架载有 389 名乘客的英国航空波音 747 客机从伦敦希思罗机场起飞，前往曼谷。它紧随其后，仅比另一架满载的 747 晚起飞两分钟。  
As the Bangkok-bound plane gained flight, climbing past a hundred feet, the pilot retracted the landing gear. At that instant, the plane’s nose abruptly pitched down, from fourteen degrees to eight degrees.  
随着飞往曼谷的飞机起飞，爬升过百英尺，飞行员收起了起落架。就在那一刻，飞机的机头突然下降，从十四度降至八度。  
The pilot immediately pulled back on the control column, and the plane continued its ascent, though at a much diminished rate.  
飞行员立即向后拉操纵杆，尽管速度大幅降低，飞机仍继续上升。  
Six to eight seconds later, the nose of the 747 pitched sharply up in response to the pilot’s continued demand on the yoke, and thereafter the controls responded normally. By his quick action, the pilot had probably averted an accident.  
六到八秒后，747 的机头在飞行员持续对操纵杆的要求下急剧向上倾斜，此后控制恢复正常。通过他的快速行动，飞行员可能避免了事故。  
Once safely aloft, the crew debated what to do next. They speculated that they might have encountered wake turbulence from the preceding 747. Since the controls now seemed to function normally, the pilot elected to go on to Bangkok, and the plane landed there without incident.  
一旦安全升空，机组人员讨论了下一步该做什么。他们推测他们可能遇到了前面 747 的尾流湍流。由于现在控制似乎正常工作，飞行员决定前往曼谷，飞机在那里着陆时没有发生事故。  
In Bangkok, engineers checked the control systems and the hydraulics, and pulled the cassette from the quick-access recorder, which contained more than two hundred flight-data parameters. They sent the cassette back to London.  
在曼谷，工程师们检查了控制系统和液压系统，并从快速访问记录器中取出磁带，该磁带包含两百多个飞行数据参数。他们将磁带送回伦敦。  
Meanwhile, since the engineers had found nothing amiss, the plane embarked on a four-day flight sequence that took it to Australia and back to Bangkok without any recurrence of the Heathrow incident.  
同时，由于工程师们没有发现任何异常，飞机开始了为期四天的飞行序列，将其从澳大利亚带回曼谷，期间没有再次发生希思罗机场事件。

In London, a British Airways team downloaded the recorder and discovered that the elevators on either side of the tail—the flight-control surfaces that determine a plane’s pitch—had moved in opposite directions.  
在伦敦，英国航空团队下载了记录器，发现飞机尾部的两侧电梯——决定飞机俯仰的飞行控制面——移动到了相反的方向。  
The left elevator had gone up, in the correctly commanded position to climb; the right one had gone down fifteen degrees, an uncommanded position that caused the plane’s nose to pitch downward.  
左电梯已上升至正确指令的爬升位置；右电梯下降了十五度，这是一个未指令的位置，导致飞机机头向下倾斜。

The airline immediately ordered the removal of the 747’s right inboard elevator’s power-control unit, a dual concentric servo valve that had been manufactured by Parker Hannifin and was strikingly similar in design and function to the one that powers rudder movements on 737s.  
航空公司立即下令拆除 747 右内侧升降舵的动力控制单元，这是一个由派克汉尼芬制造的同心双伺服阀，其设计和功能与为 737 提供舵面运动的那个非常相似。  
It sent the unit to Parker for testing. Parker found that it functioned without flaw.  
它将该单元发送到帕克进行测试。帕克发现它运行无故障。  
Boeing, which had assisted Parker, advanced a hypothesis: that a foreign object in the hydraulic fluid might have caused a momentary jam between the primary and the secondary valves, but any evidence of such a jam was lost. This report arrived on David King’s desk.  
波音公司曾协助帕克，提出了一种假设：可能是液压油中的异物导致了主阀和副阀之间的暂时堵塞，但任何此类堵塞的证据都已丢失。这份报告出现在大卫·金桌面上。  
He, his colleagues, and British Airways, unsatisfied with the explanation, demanded a more thorough investigation.  
他、他的同事以及英国航空对解释不满意，要求进行更彻底的调查。

In the end, the investigators determined that the secondary slide in the unit’s servo valve had been driven all the way to its internal stops, in the opposite direction from the primary slide, causing a cross-flow in the valve.  
最终，调查人员确定，该单元伺服阀的副滑块已被驱动至其内部止点，与主滑块方向相反，导致阀门中产生交叉流动。  
This had occurred, they theorized, either because of air in the hydraulic system, introduced during maintenance, or a surge of hydraulic fluid.  
这可能是由于维护期间引入液压系统中的空气，或者液压油的涌动造成的。  
The retraction of the landing gear had caused a pressure spike in the hydraulic system, forcing the secondary slide to its stops and the elevators in the wrong direction.  
起落架的收回导致液压系统压力骤升，迫使副滑轨停止并使升降舵朝错误方向移动。

David King felt that this event would, in all likelihood, have been written off as a wake-vortex encounter if not for a data recorder that monitored hundreds of parameters.  
大卫·金认为，如果不是因为有监控数百个参数的数据记录仪，这次事件很可能被当作一次尾涡遭遇而一笔勾销。  
What was more to the point, the event happened only once, even though the plane continued to fly for four more days before mechanics made repairs.  
更重要的是，尽管飞机在机械师进行维修之前又飞了四天，但事件只发生了一次。

It seemed entirely possible that just such a transient anomaly, elusive and maddening, had afflicted the rudder power-control unit on USAir 427. Finding such gremlins, as King testified, is difficult even with a completely intact system.  
似乎完全有可能，正是这样难以捉摸且令人抓狂的暂时性异常，影响了 USAir 427 的舵动力控制系统。正如金证词所述，即使在完全完好的系统中找到这样的小鬼也相当困难。  
Greg Phillips and his team had only fragmented systems to work with.  
Greg Phillips 和他的团队只有零散的系统可以工作。

“The best thing you can do,” Phillips said many months into the investigation, “is to find something that broke. We aren’t going to miss it if it broke. In Colorado Springs and Pittsburgh, the things we’ve found aren’t broken.”  
“你能做的最好的事情，”菲利普斯在调查进行了好几个月后说，“就是找到一些出故障的东西。如果它出了故障，我们不会错过。在科罗拉多斯普林斯和匹兹堡，我们发现的东西并没有出故障。”

Soon after the hearings, Phillips and Thomas Jacky, the head of the performance group, arranged to get data from the quick-access recorders mounted on the 737 fleet of a European airline, which records far more flight-data parameters than American carriers do.  
不久之后，菲利普斯和托马斯·杰基，表现小组的负责人，安排从一家欧洲航空公司的 737 机队上安装的快速访问记录器中获取数据，这些记录器记录的飞行数据参数比美国运营商多得多。  
Jacky searched the data for yaws or rudder anomalies of any sort. He found that the rudders rarely exceeded the three degrees of movement that fell within the authority of the yaw damper. In those few instances when they did, the pilots had always commanded the movement.  
Jacky 搜索了数据，寻找任何类型的 yaws 或舵异常。他发现舵很少超过三个度数的移动，这属于偏航阻尼器的权限范围内。在那些少数情况下，舵确实超过了这个范围，飞行员总是指挥了移动。

Many months into the investigation, Haueter sat at his desk at the Safety Board offices, across the river from National Airport, where planes rise into the air every few minutes, generating one wake vortex after another.  
许多个月份的调查中，豪特在安全委员会办公室的办公桌前坐着，位于国家机场对岸，那里每隔几分钟就有飞机升空，产生一个又一个尾流涡旋。  
He had before him a thick stack of mail from concerned citizens who, aware of the Safety Board’s perplexity, had written to offer their theories.  
他面前有一大堆来自关心市民的信件，他们意识到安全委员会的困惑，便写信提出自己的理论。

“I thought of writing you earlier,” one correspondent stated, “but delayed doing so because I thought you’d solve it.” Another called the Safety Board incompetent bunglers who couldn’t do anything. Some of the letters suggested pilot error, others saboteurs and mad bombers.  
“我本来想早点给你写信，”一位通讯员说，“但因为我以为你会解决这个问题。”另一位称安全委员会是无能的笨蛋，什么也做不了。一些信件暗示是飞行员操作失误，另一些则认为是破坏者和疯狂的炸弹客。  
A missive in bold black ink stated, “Autopilot Did It,” and, beneath that, “My opinion only.” Several writers suggested equipping planes with large parachutes to avert future high-impact crashes.  
一封用粗黑墨水书写的信件上写着：“自动驾驶干的”，下面写着：“仅代表个人观点。”几位作家建议给飞机配备大型降落伞，以避免未来发生高冲击力的事故。  
Another, having obviously read about the gruesome process of identifying victims from body parts, suggested painting passengers in different hues before permitting them to board planes.  
另一个显然读过关于从身体部位识别受害者这一恐怖过程的文章，建议在乘客登机前用不同颜色给乘客涂色。

“That’ll sell a lot of tickets,” Haueter said.  
“这会卖出很多票，”豪特说。

But he was not amused. By an edict of the chairman, who often likes to remind Safety Board employees that they are public servants, Haueter must answer all letters, and his replies must be reasoned, not perfunctory.  
但他并不觉得好笑。根据主席的命令，主席经常喜欢提醒安全委员会的员工他们是公务员，Haueter 必须回答所有信件，并且他的回复必须是有理的，而不是敷衍的。  
When he tried responding in polite, formulaic prose, he found that his correspondents would write again, and again, until they got a solid answer.  
当他试图用礼貌、公式化的散文回应时，他发现他的通信者会一次又一次地写信，直到他们得到一个明确的答案。

“We’re dealing on a level of excruciatingly small detail,” Haueter said as he went through his correspondence. “It’s hard to keep the interest level of people up.  
我们正在处理极其微小的细节，”Haueter 在查看他的信件时说。“很难保持人们的兴趣。”  
Parties to the investigation sometimes seem to have lost interest. People are saying, ‘Get on with life.’ They call it the investigation from hell around here. For me, personally, it’ll never go away.  
调查的各方有时似乎失去了兴趣。人们说，“继续生活吧。”他们把这儿的调查称为地狱般的调查。对我来说，这永远无法消失。  
Professionally, Greg and I will be chasing 737s around the world every time they have a small glitch.”  
专业上，每次他们的小故障发生时，我和格雷格都会在全球各地追逐 737。”

And so the investigation dragged on, with small clues emerging from the examination and reëxamination of every piece of evidence. The rudder-pedal assemblies for both the pilot and the first officer, for example, had been found in the wreckage.  
因此，调查持续进行，从对每一件证据的检查和重新检查中逐渐浮现出一些线索。例如，飞行员和副驾驶的舵踏板组件已在残骸中被发现。  
Although they were fragmented and heavily distorted, a Safety Board metallurgist determined that both left pedals had fractured upon impact in approximately the same place—near the pivot lugs at the top of the support tubes—whereas both right pedals, bent but intact, had remained attached to their support tubes.  
尽管它们破碎且严重变形，安全委员会的冶金学家确定，两个左踏板在撞击时在大约相同的位置——支撑管顶部的枢轴耳上——断裂，而两个右踏板虽然弯曲但完好无损，仍然连接在其支撑管上。  
Hoping that this particular pattern of damage might shed light on the crew’s use of the rudder, the human-performance group met in June of 1995 with the deputy medical examiner of the Armed Forces Institute of Pathology.  
希望这种特定的损伤模式能揭示船员使用舵的情况，人类性能小组于 1995 年 6 月与武装部队病理学研究所的副法医官会面。  
After studying the metallurgical evidence and other pertinent material, the deputy medical examiner offered one tentative explanation: “The symmetrical pattern of rudder-pedal fractures suggests a similar extension of the legs of both crew members, probably with both left legs extended and in contact with the rudder pedals, at the time of impact.”  
在研究了冶金证据和其他相关材料后，副法医提出了一个初步的解释：“舵踏板骨折的对称模式表明两名船员腿部可能以相似的方式伸展，可能是在撞击时，两腿都伸直并接触舵踏板。”

If this was true, it meant that Emmett and Germano, standing hard on the left rudder pedals, had actually steered the plane into its fatal bank. This seemed counterintuitive to almost everyone.  
如果这是真的，这意味着埃米特和德国诺，站在左舵踏板上，实际上已经将飞机驶入了致命的倾斜。这几乎对每个人来说都显得不合常理。  
It met with heated debate, even derision, among some investigators—especially those from USAir and the pilots’ union. Haueter, in the cause of leaving no stone unturned, pronounced it “interesting.” It was, after all, a fact that the rudder pedals had been sheared.  
它在一些调查人员中引起了热烈的争论，甚至嘲笑——尤其是来自 USAir 和飞行员工会的那些人。豪特在力求不遗漏任何细节的过程中，称其为“有趣的”。毕竟，舵踏板被剪断的事实是存在的。  
But he reserved judgment on the extended-leg theory. It seemed far more likely that the pedals had sheared because of the angle of the descent—nose slightly to the left—and the pilots’ bracing themselves for impact.  
但他对延长腿理论保留了判断。看起来更有可能是因为下降角度——鼻子略微向左——以及飞行员为撞击做准备，导致踏板断裂。

This extended-leg theory seemed to some like a reading of entrails—a sign, perhaps, that the investigation was foundering. But Haueter was not without a plan.  
这一延长腿理论在一些人看来像是对内脏的解读——或许是一个迹象，表明调查正在陷入困境。但豪特有计划。  
After lengthy consultation with his superiors at the Safety Board, he decided to stage a wake-vortex test using a 727 and a 737 configured in the same manner as the Delta and the USAir planes on the day of the accident.  
在与安全委员会上级长时间协商后，他决定使用一架 727 和一架 737 进行类似事故当天达美航空和美国航空飞机的配置进行尾流涡旋测试。  
This plan struck some as worthy and others as a waste of time and money: after all, wake-vortex encounters were supposedly common occurrences for line pilots. Haueter, for his part, felt he could not consider the investigation complete without such a test.  
这个计划有些人认为值得，有些人则认为浪费时间和金钱：毕竟，对于航线飞行员来说，遭遇尾流涡流似乎是常见的事。Haueter 本人则认为，没有这样的测试，他无法认为调查是完整的。

At the F.A.A. Technical Center, at the Atlantic City airport, the investigators rigged the wingtips of an F.A.A.-owned 727 with smoke generators to mark the wake vortices.  
在 F.A.A.技术中心，大西洋城机场，调查人员用烟雾发生器装备了一架 F.A.A.拥有的 727 飞机的翼尖，以标记尾流涡旋。  
They outfitted the 737, on loan from USAir, with advanced flight-data and cockpit voice recorders and with seven video cameras. As the week of the test drew near, speculation among the investigators about what they would find grew heated.  
他们为从 USAir 借来的 737 配备了先进的飞行数据和驾驶舱语音记录仪，以及七个摄像头。随着测试周的到来，调查人员对他们会发现什么的猜测变得越来越激烈。  
Some were convinced the wake vortex, properly intercepted, would cause the 737 to yaw dramatically. Others were equally certain that wouldn’t happen. Haueter tried to keep an open mind, but on balance he sided with the skeptics.  
有些人相信，如果正确拦截，尾流涡旋会导致 737 飞机急剧偏航。其他人同样确信这不会发生。豪特试图保持开放的心态，但总的来说，他站在怀疑者一边。

The tests started on September 25th and ran for seven days. The test pilots, from the F.A.A., Boeing, and USAir, flew once, and sometimes twice a day. They flew toward the wake of the 727 more than a hundred and sixty times.  
测试于 9 月 25 日开始，持续了七天。来自美国联邦航空管理局（F.A.A.）、波音公司和 USAir 的测试飞行员每天飞行一次，有时两次。他们超过一百六十次飞向 727 的尾流。  
Both the investigators and the test pilots, able to see for the first time the twin vortices coming off either wingtip of the 727, were astonished at their durability.  
研究人员和试飞员首次看到 727 机翼尖处产生的双涡旋，对其持久性感到惊讶。  
In calm weather, they remained tightly coiled, with a core diameter of between four and five feet, for four or five miles or more.  
在平静的天气里，它们紧紧地盘绕着，核心直径在四到五英尺之间，长达四五英里或更远。  
The vortices typically descended about three hundred feet behind the 727—the exact difference in altitude between USAir 427 and the Delta 727 at the moment of upset—and remained there until they dissipated.  
涡流通常下降到 727 后方约 300 英尺——这是 USAir 427 和 Delta 727 在失控时刻的精确高度差——并一直停留在那里，直到消散。

The F.A.A. test pilot, Lester Berven, testifying at a second Safety Board hearing about his experience during the vortex test, said, “We put the airplane into the vortex in every position that could be done, wingwise and tailwise and fuselagewise.  
美国联邦航空管理局试飞员莱斯特·贝文在第二次安全委员会听证会上就他在涡流测试中的经历作证时表示：“我们将飞机置于涡流中的每一个可能的位置，包括翼向、尾向和机身尾部。”  
We went up and down and crosswise, and rolling and non-rolling. We stayed in the vortex for half an hour total, right in the middle of one or two, or both of them.”  
我们上下左右，滚动和非滚动。我们总共在涡流中停留了半小时，正好在其中一个或两个，或者两个中间。

Asked by the board of inquiry whether any of the vortex encounters had created any upset, Berven replied, “No, there was very little effect on pitch or yaw. There was some turbulence, of course, but no sustained pitch or yaw at all. . . .  
询问调查委员会是否任何涡流遭遇造成了任何干扰，贝文回答道：“没有，对俯仰或偏航的影响非常小。当然有一些湍流，但完全没有持续的俯仰或偏航……”  
You can just pull right up in there with absolute impunity and fly anywhere you want with respect to either one or both of those vortices without a controllability problem.”  
您可以毫无顾忌地直接飞进去，无论是对其中一个还是两个涡旋，都可以随意飞行，而不会出现可控性问题。”

The USAir pilot, John Cox, also testified. “We put several parts of the airplane carefully in the vortex, looking for yaw,” he said. “Particularly with the fin only in the [vortex] core, and with the yaw damper off, I had expected a fair amount of yaw, and it wasn’t there.”  
美国航空飞行员约翰·科克斯也作了证。“我们将飞机的几个部分小心地放入涡流中，寻找偏航，”他说。“特别是只有水平尾翼在[涡流]核心中，而且偏航阻尼器关闭，我本以为会有相当多的偏航，但并没有。”

Nor did Berven or Cox, who was a professional pilot of twenty-two years’ experience, find the vortex encounters startling or disorienting. “It was consistent pretty much with things we see on the line,” Cox said.  
贝文和科克斯也没有发现涡流遭遇令人震惊或迷失方向。科克斯说：“这基本上与我们在线上看到的东西一致。”  
“Operating in and out of the East Coast, we go into high-density airports with great frequency. On a three-day trip, it’s normal to come across the wake vortex of traffic. . . . In a line operation, you never know they’re coming. . . .  
在东海岸运营，我们频繁进出高密度机场。在三天行程中，遇到交通尾流漩涡是正常的...在航线操作中，你永远不知道它们何时到来...  
I don’t know if it’s [a] surprise, because it rapidly goes to identifying what it is and going on. . . . It doesn’t cause a reflex action or grave concern.”  
我不知道这是否是个惊喜，因为它迅速地识别出它是什么，并继续下去……它不会引起反射动作或严重关切。”

In contrast, the Boeing test pilot, replying to questions put to him by Boeing’s director of air-safety investigation, was prompted to agree that wake-vortex encounters had the potential to startle, confuse, and even threaten pilots.  
相比之下，波音的试飞员在回答波音航空安全调查部经理提出的问题时，被促使同意认为尾流涡流遭遇有可能令人惊吓、困惑，甚至威胁到飞行员。

As it turned out, the investigator who had expected the least new information from the wake-vortex test got the most. Jim Cash reviewed the cockpit-voice-recorder tapes from all the test flights.  
结果证明，原本预期从尾流涡测试中获得最少新信息的调查员却得到了最多。吉姆·卡什审查了所有测试飞行的驾驶舱语音记录带。  
On the first day of the test, one of the pilots reported hearing sounds as the plane intercepted the wake vortex of the 727. This pilot, however, did not think that the sounds were the same as the muted thumps on the USAir 427 tape.  
测试的第一天，一名飞行员报告说，当飞机拦截 727 的尾流涡旋时，他听到了声音。然而，这位飞行员并不认为这些声音与 USAir 427 磁带上的沉闷的砰砰声相同。

But when Cash listened to the test plane’s tape, the thumps sounded to him “identical” to those on the USAir 427 tape. This wasn’t just a matter of Cash’s finely tuned ear.  
但是当 Cash 听了测试飞机的录音带时，他听到的砰砰声在他听来“完全相同”于 USAir 427 录音带上的声音。这不仅仅是 Cash 敏锐的耳朵的问题。  
The test pilot had heard only the airborne sounds, whereas the voice recorder also captured the sounds transmitted through the plane’s structure. “The structure-borne sounds give the wake thumps a more bassy resonance than when you just hear them,” Cash explained.  
测试飞行员只听到了空中声音，而录音机还捕捉到了通过飞机结构传输的声音。“结构传声使得尾流拍击声比仅听到它们时更有低沉的共鸣，”卡什解释道。  
Moreover, when USAir 427’s thumps were plotted on a sound-frequency graph, they looked identical to the thumps of the test plane.  
此外，当 USAir 427 的轰鸣声在声频图上绘制时，它们看起来与测试飞机的轰鸣声完全相同。

The thumps occurred only when the test plane intercepted the vortices at certain angles. This gave the test pilots a heightened awareness of cockpit noises. The Boeing pilot noticed that the cockpit windshield wipers lifted off the windshield during certain vortex intercepts.  
当测试飞机以特定角度拦截涡流时，才会发生这种撞击声。这使得测试飞行员对驾驶舱噪音有了更高的警觉性。波音飞行员注意到，在拦截某些涡流时，驾驶舱风挡雨刷会从风挡上抬起。  
He believed that they might be the source of the mysterious clickety-clicks. Cash, however, remained uncommitted. The test recorder did not capture those sounds; it captured only the Boeing pilot saying, “There was the click.”  
他认为这些声音可能是神秘“嘀嘀”声的来源。然而，凯西仍然保持中立。测试记录器没有捕捉到那些声音；它只捕捉到了波音飞行员说：“那里有嘀嘀声。”

In his lab at the Safety Board, some months after the wake-vortex test, Cash said, “I don’t think I can squeeze a lot more out of the Pittsburgh tape. There are still some sounds that are unknown. One, in particular, still nags at me. It has body to it, resonance.  
在他的安全委员会的实验室里，在醒涡测试几个月后，Cash 说：“我觉得我不能再从匹兹堡磁带上榨取更多东西了。还有一些声音是未知的。特别是有一个声音仍然困扰着我。它有身体，有共鸣。”  
There are lots of metal-to-metal impulse sounds, like a seat belt snapping shut or a switch being thrown. This one isn’t like that.  
有很多金属与金属的冲击声，比如安全带突然关闭或开关被抛掷的声音。这个不是那样的。  
It occurs just after he says ‘Jetstream,’ between that and the thump, thump, thump, right around when he says ‘Sheez’—during those four seconds when the upset began. It’s in a very bad spot, as far as not providing any idea what it is.  
它发生在他说“Jetstream”之后，在那之后和砰砰砰砰之间，就在他说“我的天”的时候——就在那四个秒开始不满的时候。就其不提供任何关于它是什么的想法而言，它处于一个非常糟糕的位置。  
There’s a lot of noise in cockpits, and, experience has shown, most of it doesn’t mean anything. It sounds like maybe a briefcase or a suitcase falling over. But I haven’t heard it before. It’s unusual, and it’s in a bad spot.”  
驾驶舱里噪音很多，经验表明，其中大部分都没有意义。听起来可能是一只公文包或手提箱掉落的声音。但我以前没听过。这很奇怪，而且位置很不好。

The wake-vortex test provided interesting information, but it failed to yield any hard evidence concerning the cause of the crash. Haueter had no choice but to press doggedly on.  
该尾涡测试提供了有趣的信息，但未能提供任何关于事故原因的硬性证据。豪特只能坚持不懈地继续下去。  
Last January, when a twenty-seven-year-old 737 was retired from service and destined for a museum of flight, he and Phillips jumped at the chance to work on it. Haueter, Phillips, and the rest of the systems group conceived a new battery of tests for the rudder system.  
去年一月，当一架 27 岁的 737 飞机退役并注定要进入飞行博物馆时，他和菲利普斯抓住了这个机会来工作。豪特、菲利普斯和系统组的其他成员为舵系统构想了一系列新的测试。  
Haueter admitted that these were “really extreme tests, extreme limits, almost bizarre tests,” which could be conducted only on an Iron Bird—a plane never meant to fly again—because of their destructive nature.  
豪特承认，这些是“真正极端的测试，极端的极限，几乎怪异的测试”，因为它们的破坏性，只能在“铁鸟”——一架从未打算再次飞行的飞机——上进行。  
Among other things, they subjected the rudder section to repetitive swings; they cut rudder cables to see what the snap sounded like; they jammed the standby rudder actuator and jammed it again; they took up the aisle flooring at the front of the plane—the “soft and spongy” spot noted by maintenance on USAir 427—and pressed increasingly heavy loads on the rudder cables.  
他们还对舵叶部分进行了重复摆动；他们剪断了舵缆，以听一下断裂的声音；他们卡住了备用舵机，然后再次卡住；他们抬起了飞机前部的过道地板——“软而多孔”的区域，由维护人员在 USAir 427 上标记——并在舵缆上施加越来越重的负荷。  
The rudder, of course, did move, but only fractionally. Having tested “the outer limits of probability,” as Phillips put it, the investigators, in the end, did not find the results especially fruitful.  
当然，舵确实移动了，但只是微乎其微。正如菲利普斯所说，经过测试“概率的外部极限”，调查人员最终并没有发现结果特别富有成效。

When, in April, the plane carrying Secretary of Commerce Ron Brown—a military version of the 737—crashed in Croatia, Haueter and Phillips went to investigate.  
四月份，载有商务部长罗恩·布朗的飞机——一款军用 737 版本——在克罗地亚坠毁时，豪特和菲利普斯前往调查。  
In itself, this was unusual, for the military guards its own crash investigations closely, rarely requesting the presence of the Safety Board. But the accident shed no light on Pittsburgh or on Colorado Springs.  
本身，这很罕见，因为军方对其坠机调查监管严格，很少要求安全委员会在场。但这次事故并未为匹兹堡或科罗拉多斯普林斯带来任何线索。  
It appeared that bad weather, outdated navigational equipment, and pilot error had caused that tragedy.  
天气恶劣、导航设备过时和飞行员操作失误导致了这场悲剧。

And then, on the morning of June 10th, Haueter heard about an incident that seemed much more pertinent to the Flight 427 investigation.  
然后，在 6 月 10 日的早晨，豪特听到一起似乎与 427 航班调查更为相关的突发事件。  
The night before, a Boeing 737 owned by Eastwind Airlines, a small new carrier, had begun its approach to Richmond when the pilot felt a sudden movement of the rudder—a “bump”—and the plane rolled hard to the right.  
前一天晚上，一家小型新航空公司 Eastwind Airlines 的一架波音 737 在飞往里士满时，飞行员突然感觉到舵面有剧烈的“颠簸”动作，飞机向右剧烈滚转。  
The pilot declared an emergency and, as he later told Haueter, believed he had stood on the left rudder pedal and used asymmetric thrust to bring the plane back to level flight. Haueter and Phillips and investigators from Boeing quickly convened in Richmond to examine the plane.  
飞行员宣布了紧急情况，后来他告诉豪特，他认为是站在了左舵踏板上，并使用了不对称推力将飞机恢复到水平飞行。豪特、菲利普斯和波音的调查员迅速在里士满召集，以检查该飞机。  
The maintenance records told a story eerily similar to Colorado Springs—three previous complaints of rudder-control anomalies within the preceding month. In each instance, mechanics had replaced parts of the rudder system.  
维护记录讲述了一个与科罗拉多斯普林斯惊人相似的故事——在上个月，有三次关于舵控制异常的投诉。在每一次事件中，机械师都更换了舵系统的一部分。

Thus far, Haueter’s investigation into the Eastwind incident has turned up a misadjusted yaw damper. He and Phillips have plans for more tests, but the incident, to Haueter’s dismay, has not yet shed any new light on Pittsburgh.  
截至目前，豪特对东风事件的调查发现了一个失调的偏航阻尼器。他和菲利普斯计划进行更多测试，但令豪特沮丧的是，这一事件尚未为匹兹堡带来任何新的线索。

Not long ago, in a meeting at the Safety Board, a colleague not involved in the investigation said to Phillips during a heated exchange, “You’ve worked both Colorado Springs and Pittsburgh. Obviously, you can’t figure an accident out.”  
不久前，在一次安全委员会的会议上，一位未参与调查的同事在与菲利普斯的激烈争论中说：“你在科罗拉多斯普林斯和匹兹堡都工作过。显然，你无法弄清楚事故的原因。”

The words were spoken in frustration, during discussion of an event unrelated to the investigation, but Phillips acknowledges that they are true. “There’s a tremendous amount of pressure, within myself and from outside, to find out what happened.  
这些话是在挫败中说的，在讨论与调查无关的事件期间，但菲利普斯承认这些话是真实的。“我自己和外界都承受着巨大的压力，要找出发生了什么。”  
I want to be certain I’ve looked at everything possible. I want to make sure there’s nothing we can do that hasn’t been done. I’m glad I worked Colorado Springs, or else I’d be wondering about it. On the other hand, since I worked both, I wonder about how people look at me.”  
我想确保我已经看过所有可能的东西。我想确保我们没有做过的事情都已经做了。我很高兴我在科罗拉多斯普林斯工作过，否则我还在想这件事。另一方面，既然我两个都工作过，我在想人们是如何看待我的。”

Haueter knows the feeling. “You’ll always be the one who didn’t do it,” he says simply.  
豪特知道这种感觉。“你永远都是那个没做的人，”他简单地说。

In the end, the investigation of Flight 427 has come down to this: the yaw, the ensuing roll, and the crash occurred as the result of either a malfunction in Boeing’s rudder system or an error on the part of the pilots, or a combination of both.  
最终，427 航班调查的结果归结为以下几点：偏航、随后发生的滚转以及坠机是由波音的舵系统故障、飞行员操作失误，或者两者兼而有之造成的。

The great majority of accidents—seventy-five per cent or more by most calculations—are attributed to human error on the part of pilots, air-traffic controllers, or maintenance crews.  
大多数事故——根据大多数计算，达到 75%或更多——归因于飞行员、空中交通管制员或维护人员的人为错误。  
Given these figures, the Air Line Pilots Association has reason for concern that blame for the accident will eventually fall on Emmett and Germano.  
鉴于这些数据，航空线飞行员协会有理由担心，最终事故的责任将落在埃米特和德国诺身上。

None of the parties to the investigation have stated publicly what they believe to be the cause of the crash, but it is not difficult to detect the tenor of Boeing’s sentiments.  
调查的任何一方都没有公开表示他们认为的坠机原因，但并不难察觉波音的情感基调。  
In the public hearings, Boeing has several times urged the Safety Board to place more emphasis on the human-performance aspects of the crash.  
在公开听证会上，波音多次敦促安全委员会更加重视事故中的人类表现方面。  
A report recently presented to the human-performance group by two Boeing investigators—one of them the test pilot who had flown many of the wake-vortex encounters, the other a former nasa research psychologist—consisted largely of a detailed docket of many previous incidents of pilot error, implying that some precedents existed for a scenario in which Emmett and Germano would have caused the crash.  
最近提交给人机性能小组的一份报告，由两名波音调查员提出——其中一位是曾驾驶过许多尾流涡旋遭遇的试飞员，另一位是前 NASA 研究心理学家——主要包含许多先前飞行员错误事件的详细记录，暗示了埃米特和杰尔马诺可能造成坠机的事故场景存在先例。  
The group declined to accept this report, which some felt contained more opinion than might be warranted at that stage. In the absence of conclusive proof that its rudder system failed, Boeing clearly seeks to direct attention toward the pilots and away from its planes.  
该小组拒绝接受这份报告，有些人认为其中包含的意见可能比当时应有的要多。在没有确凿证据证明其舵系统失效的情况下，波音显然试图将注意力引向飞行员，而不是其飞机。

Haueter, who will not discuss the contents of the report, acknowledges only this: “Some people—Boeing people, Safety Board people—would argue that the pilots got caught in the wake vortex and reacted badly.”  
豪特，不愿讨论报告内容，仅承认这一点：“有些人——波音公司的人，安全委员会的人——会认为飞行员被卷入了尾流涡流并做出了不良反应。”

The Air Line Pilots Association is perfectly aware of this argument. One union representative said, “Our fear is that they’ll try to fit a square peg into a round hole. We’re the square peg.”  
空中航线飞行员协会完全清楚这个论点。一位工会代表说：“我们的担忧是他们试图把一个方钉塞进一个圆孔。我们就是那个方钉。”

Some interested observers who took no part in the investigation have made known what they believe to be the cause.  
一些未参与调查的感兴趣观察者已经公布他们认为的原因。  
Arthur Wolk, a Philadelphia lawyer who specializes in aviation disasters and represents the families of a victim of Colorado Springs and several from USAir 427, has amassed an extensive file on the 737 rudder system.  
亚瑟·沃尔克，一位专长于航空事故的费城律师，代表科罗拉多斯普林斯的一名受害者以及美国航空 427 航班的多位家属，积累了大量关于 737 方向舵系统的文件。  
“There’s no question that the cause is the same in both cases,” Wolk asserts. “This plane has a long history of rudder-control anomalies . . . instances of the rudder going to positions not selected by the flight crew.  
“毫无疑问，这两个案例的原因是相同的，”沃尔克断言。“这架飞机有着长期舵控制异常的历史……舵被飞行机组人员未选择的位置。”  
I knew Pittsburgh was a rudder hardover from the evening of the accident.”  
我知道从事故当晚起，匹兹堡就陷入了困境。”

Wolk is not reticent about expressing his opinion. He did so last November, when the Safety Board chairman, Jim Hall, convened the second public hearing into USAir 427, and asked each of the parties if any aspect of the crash should be explored more thoroughly.  
Wolk 并不害羞地表达自己的观点。他去年 11 月就是这样做的，当时安全委员会主席 Jim Hall 召开了关于 USAir 427 的第二场公开听证会，并询问各方是否应该更深入地调查事故的任何方面。  
Standing before television cameras in a corridor outside the hearing room, Wolk voiced his disdain for the Safety Board’s investigation: “You heard Jim Hall asking Boeing what they should be doing? The head of the Safety Board asking Boeing what it should be doing!  
站在听证室外的走廊里，沃尔克表达了他对安全委员会调查的不满：“你们听到吉姆·霍尔询问波音他们应该做什么了吗？安全委员会的头头询问波音他们应该做什么！”  
That is appalling!”  那太可怕了！”

In his more charitable moments, Wolk concedes that the Safety Board is well intentioned—just seriously myopic. “They’re looking for a broken part,” he explains. “The problem is, you can’t find a broken part. It’s a design problem that allows the rudder to go hardover.  
在他较为仁慈的时刻，Wolk 承认安全委员会是善意的——只是严重缺乏远见。“他们在寻找一个损坏的部件，”他解释道。“问题是，你找不到一个损坏的部件。这是一个允许舵过度转向的设计问题。”  
You have two components of the power-control unit that have to slide perfectly freely under all circumstances and for the life of the plane. The tolerance for these valves is a fraction of the size of a human hair. You don’t need much to make it not work.  
您有两个功率控制单元组件，它们必须在所有情况下以及整个飞机寿命内完美自由滑动。这些阀门的公差仅是人体头发的几分之一。让它不工作不需要太多。  
Even a momentary binding of the slide can cause the plane to roll upside down before the pilots can ever react.”  
即使是一瞬间的滑行绑定也可能导致飞机在飞行员反应之前翻滚到倒立状态。”

Modern jet airplanes are designed with highly redundant systems, which make accidents highly improbable.  
现代喷气式飞机设计有高度冗余的系统，这使得事故极不可能发生。  
When they do occur, they are usually the result of a concatenation of discrete events—of mechanical or human failures—any one of which by itself would not likely cause a catastrophe.  
当它们发生时，通常是由一系列离散事件——机械或人为故障——的连续发生造成的——任何一个单独的事件都不太可能引发灾难。  
It is this unforeseen sequence of events, resulting from what accident theorists call the “tight coupling” of complex interacting systems, that causes accidents.  
这是由事故理论家所说的复杂相互作用系统“紧密耦合”导致的一系列不可预见的事件，正是这些事件引发了事故。  
In the past thirty years, as aviation technology has grown more sophisticated—and therefore more tightly coupled—it has also grown safer. But those gains in safety have tended to erode under demands that planes fly faster, higher, and in more severe weather conditions.  
在过去的三十年里，随着航空技术的日益复杂——因此也更加紧密耦合——它也变得更加安全。但这些安全方面的收益往往会在飞机要求飞得更快、更高以及更恶劣的天气条件下而逐渐减弱。  
The F.A.A., which has the conflicting mandate of promoting air travel as it polices the air-travel industry, announced a goal of zero accidents early last year. This, of course, sounds like a laudable goal.  
美国联邦航空管理局（F.A.A.）在监管航空业的同时，肩负着推广航空旅行的使命，去年初宣布了一个零事故的目标。当然，这听起来是一个值得赞扬的目标。  
Perhaps it is just meant to reassure a worried public, for almost no one—and least of all the investigators at the Major Investigations Division of the Safety Board—believes that such a goal is attainable.  
或许这只是为了安抚担忧的公众，因为几乎没有人——尤其是安全委员会重大调查部门的调查员——相信这样的目标是可实现的。

The events that led to the crash of Flight 427 and killed a hundred and thirty-two people on September 8, 1994, lasted only twenty-six seconds from the moment of the initial upset to the moment of impact.  
导致 1994 年 9 月 8 日 427 航班坠毁并造成 132 人死亡的事件，从最初的不稳定到撞击的整个过程仅持续了 26 秒。  
Of those twenty-six seconds, only about eight seconds are crucial to the understanding of what occurred. After those first eight seconds, the pilots had become passengers.  
在这二十六秒中，只有大约八秒对于理解所发生的事情至关重要。在那最初的八秒之后，飞行员已经变成了乘客。

Almost two years after the crash, Tom Haueter still hopes he will find the solution. But at some point he and Phillips and the other investigators might have to admit that they have learned all that it is humanly possible to learn from the wreckage.  
近两年后，汤姆·豪特仍希望他能找到解决方案。但到了某个时候，他和菲利普斯以及其他调查人员可能不得不承认，他们已经从残骸中了解了所有人类可能了解的一切。  
At that point, what happened to Flight 427 will become just another mystery among the many we must live with. ♦  
在那个时刻，427 航班发生了什么将变成我们必须与之共存的众多谜团之一。
