Bookshelf  书架
-------------

I sometimes post book recommendations on Twitter and people often respond asking if I have an overall list of books I think are worth reading. I don&#39;t, and end up feeling a bit guilty about being so haphazard.  
我有时会在推特上发布书籍推荐，人们经常回应问我是否有我认为值得一读的书籍总列表。我没有，最后觉得自己有点儿随意，有点儿内疚。  
So, maybe this will be interesting to some people: below is a fairly complete catalog of the physical books I own. I always enjoy reading others&#39; lists and browsing their bookshelves, so this intended to be a digital version of that. I&#39;ve only read about half of the books here.  
所以，这可能对某些人很有趣：下面是我拥有的实体书的相当完整的目录。我总是喜欢阅读别人的列表并浏览他们的书架，所以这打算成为一个数字版本。我这里只读过一半的书。  
(I guess I fall somewhere in the middle in the [Umberto Eco theory of the library](https://books.google.com/books?id&#61;_ntDTaMUys8C&amp;lpg&#61;PT115&amp;ots&#61;E3jhWnG6aB&amp;pg&#61;PT124#v&#61;onepage&amp;q&amp;f&#61;false).)  
（我认为我在乌尔贝托·艾可的图书馆理论中处于中间位置。）

I flagged a few books that I thought were particularly great in green and those that I thought were substantially above average in light blue. These designations are non-exhaustive, though—it&#39;s kinda hard to recommend books in the abstract. And I&#39;ve no doubt missed some.  
我标记了几本我认为特别出色的绿色书籍和那些我认为远超平均水平的浅蓝色书籍。这些分类并不全面——推荐书籍时很难做到具体而微。而且我肯定遗漏了一些。

Since the stacks and shelves lack any particular order, so too does this list.  
由于堆叠和货架没有任何特定的顺序，因此这个列表也是如此。
