A Beginner&#39;s Guide to Azure&#39;s Free Text-to-Speech Service  

Azure 免费文本转语音服务入门指南

Summary 摘要

This guide provides a step-by-step walkthrough for non-technical users on how to set up and use the free tier of Microsoft Azure&#39;s Text-to-Speech (TTS) service for personal projects.  

本指南为非技术用户提供了分步操作指南，介绍如何为个人项目设置并使用 Microsoft Azure 文本转语音（TTS）服务的免费层级。  

You will learn how to create a free account, generate high-quality audio without writing any code, and understand the usage limits so you never have to worry about unexpected charges.  

您将学习如何创建一个免费账户，在无需编写任何代码的情况下生成高质量音频，并了解使用限制，从而再也不用担心产生意外费用。

- **Setting Up Your Free Account:** Learn how to sign up for a free Azure account and create the necessary &quot;Speech service&quot; resource.
设置您的免费账户：了解如何注册一个免费的 Azure 账户，并创建所需的“语音服务”资源。
- **Generating Audio with Speech Studio:** A simple, no-code method to turn your text into natural-sounding speech using an intuitive web-based tool.
使用 Speech Studio 生成音频：一种简单、无需编码的方法，通过直观的基于网页的工具将您的文本转换为自然流畅的语音。
- **Understanding Your Free Usage:** A clear breakdown of what you get for free each month and what happens if you reach the limit.
了解您的免费使用额度：清晰说明您每月可免费获得的服务内容，以及超出限额后会发生什么。
- **Tips and Troubleshooting:** Find answers to common problems and tips to ensure a smooth experience.
提示与故障排除：查找常见问题的解答及确保顺畅体验的实用技巧。

Part 1: Setting Up Your Free Azure Account and Speech Service  

第一部分：设置您的免费 Azure 帐户和语音服务

Getting started with Azure involves two main steps: creating a general Azure account and then activating the specific Speech service within it.  

开始使用 Azure 涉及两个主要步骤：首先创建一个通用的 Azure 帐户，然后在其中激活特定的语音服务。

Azure requires a free account to access any of its services. This account includes access to many &quot;always free&quot; services, including the Text-to-Speech tier.  

Azure 要求您注册一个免费帐户才能访问其任何服务。该帐户包含许多“永久免费”服务的访问权限，其中包括文本转语音服务层级。

1. **Visit the Azure Website:** Navigate to the official [Azure free account page](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/).
访问 Azure 网站：导航至 Azure 官方免费帐户页面。
2. **Start the Signup Process:** Click on the &quot;Start free&quot; or &quot;Try Azure for free&quot; button. You will be prompted to sign in with a Microsoft account (such as an [Outlook.com](http://Outlook.com), Hotmail, or [Live.com](http://Live.com) email address). If you don&#39;t have one, you can create one for free.
开始注册流程：点击“免费开始”或“免费试用 Azure”按钮。系统将提示您使用 Microsoft 帐户（例如 [Outlook.com](http://Outlook.com)、Hotmail 或 [Live.com](http://Live.com) 邮箱地址）登录。如果您还没有 Microsoft 帐户，可以免费创建一个。
3. **Verify Your Identity:** You will need to provide a phone number and a credit or debit card for identity verification.
验证您的身份：您需要提供一个电话号码以及一张信用卡或借记卡以完成身份验证。
  - **A Note on the Credit Card Requirement:** This is often a point of concern for new users. Azure uses this to verify you are a real person and to prevent abuse of their free services. A temporary authorization hold (usually $1) may be placed on your card but will be removed. You will **not** be charged unless you manually decide to upgrade your account to a &quot;pay-as-you-go&quot; subscription later.
关于信用卡要求的说明：这通常是新用户比较关心的一个问题。Azure 使用此信息来验证您是真实用户，并防止滥用其免费服务。您的卡上可能会暂时冻结一小笔金额（通常为 1 美元），但随后会解除冻结。除非您之后手动决定将账户升级为“按使用量付费”订阅，否则不会产生任何费用。

Once your account is set up, you need to &quot;turn on&quot; the specific Text-to-Speech service. In Azure, services are called &quot;resources.&quot;  

账户设置完成后，您需要“启用”特定的文本转语音服务。在 Azure 中，这些服务被称为“资源”。

1. **Navigate the Azure Portal:** After signing up, you will be taken to the **Azure portal**, which is your main dashboard.
导航 Azure 门户：注册后，您将进入 Azure 门户，这是您的主要仪表板。
2. **Find the Speech Service:** In the main search bar at the top of the portal, type &quot; **Speech** &quot; and select &quot; **Speech services** &quot; from the list that appears.
查找语音服务：在门户顶部的主搜索栏中，输入“Speech”，然后从出现的列表中选择“Speech services”。
3. **Create the Resource:** Click the &quot; **+ Create** &quot; button on the Speech services page.
创建资源：在语音服务页面上，单击“+ 创建”按钮。
4. **Fill out the Form:** You will see a form with several fields. Here’s how to fill them out for the free tier:
填写表格：您将看到一个包含多个字段的表单。以下是为免费层级填写这些字段的方法：
  - **Subscription:** Select &quot;Free Trial&quot; or the name of your subscription.
订阅：选择“免费试用”或您的订阅名称。
  - **Resource group:** A resource group is like a folder for your project. Click &quot; **Create new** &quot; and give it a simple name (e.g., &#96;MySpeechProjects&#96;).
资源组：资源组就像您项目的文件夹。单击“新建”，并为其取一个简单的名称（例如， &#96;MySpeechProjects&#96; ）。
  - **Region:** Choose a location that is geographically close to you.
区域：请选择一个在地理位置上离您较近的地点。
  - **Name:** Give your resource a unique name (e.g., &#96;MyPersonalTTS&#96;).
名称：为您的资源取一个唯一的名称（例如， &#96;MyPersonalTTS&#96; ）。
  - **Pricing tier:** This is the most important step. Click &quot; **Change** &quot; if necessary and select the **Free F0** tier.
定价层级：这是最重要的一步。如有需要，单击“更改”并选择免费的 F0 层级。
5. **Deploy the Service:** Click the &quot; **Review + create** &quot; button at the bottom, and then click &quot; **Create**.&quot; It may take a minute or two for Azure to get your service ready.
部署服务：单击底部的“查看 + 创建”按钮，然后单击“创建”。Azure 可能需要一两分钟来准备好您的服务。

Part 2: Generating Audio with Speech Studio (The No-Code Way)  

第二部分：使用语音工作室生成音频（无代码方式）

The easiest way to use the service is through **Speech Studio**, a web-based tool that lets you generate audio without touching a single line of code.  

使用该服务最简单的方式是通过语音工作室，这是一个基于网页的工具，无需编写任何代码即可生成音频。

1. **Go to Speech Studio:** Open the [Speech Studio website](https://speech.microsoft.com/) in your browser.
进入语音工作室：在浏览器中打开语音工作室网站。
2. **Sign In:** Sign in with the same Microsoft account you used for Azure. You may be asked to select the Speech resource you just created.
登录：使用您用于 Azure 的同一微软账户登录。系统可能会要求您选择刚刚创建的语音资源。
3. **Open the Audio Content Creation Tool:** On the Speech Studio homepage, find the card that says &quot; **Audio Content Creation** &quot; and click on it. This is a user-friendly tool designed for creating speech from text.
打开音频内容创建工具：在语音工作室主页上，找到标有“音频内容创建”的卡片并点击。这是一个用户友好的工具，旨在将文本转换为语音。
4. **Create Your Audio:创建您的音频：**
  - Click &quot; **New** &quot; and select &quot; **Text file**.&quot;
点击“新建”，然后选择“文本文件”。
  - A text editor will open. Type or paste the text you want to convert into speech.
将打开一个文本编辑器。输入或粘贴您想要转换成语音的文本。
  - On the right-hand side, you can choose the **Language** and **Voice**. There are hundreds of natural-sounding &quot;Neural&quot; voices to choose from.
在右侧，您可以选择语言和声音。这里有数百种自然逼真的“Neural”语音可供选择。
  - You can click the play button next to each voice to hear a sample. Once you select a voice, you can also adjust its **Style** (e.g., cheerful, empathetic), **Speaking rate**, and **Pitch**.
您可以点击每个语音旁边的播放按钮来收听示例。选择语音后，您还可以调整其风格（例如，欢快、共情）、语速和音高。
5. **Preview and Download:预览与下载：**
  - Once you&#39;ve written your text and selected a voice, click the main **Play** icon at the top to hear a preview of the full audio.
在您输入文本并选择好语音后，点击顶部的主要播放图标，即可收听完整音频的预览效果。
  - When you&#39;re happy with the result, click the &quot; **Export** &quot; button. You can then download the audio directly to your computer, typically as an MP3 file.
当您对结果满意时，点击“导出”按钮。随后，您可以将音频直接下载到您的电脑上，通常会以 MP3 文件的形式保存。

Part 3: Understanding Your Free Usage  

第三部分：了解您的免费使用额度

The Azure free tier is generous for personal use, but it&#39;s helpful to know the limits.  

Azure 免费层级对个人使用来说非常慷慨，但了解其限制也很有帮助。

With the **Free F0** pricing tier, you get:  

通过免费的 F0 定价层级，您可以获得：

- **0.5 million characters** of **Neural TTS** per month. Neural voices are the highest quality and most human-like. This is equivalent to roughly 7-8 hours of spoken audio.
每月可使用 50 万个字符的神经网络 TTS。神经网络语音质量最高，最接近真人发音。这大约相当于 7 到 8 小时的音频内容。

You will **not** be automatically charged. If you use up your 0.5 million characters before the end of the monthly cycle, the service will simply stop working.  

您不会被自动扣费。如果您在当月周期结束前用完了这 50 万个字符，服务将直接停止运行。  

You might receive an error message (like HTTP 429: Too Many Requests) until your free allowance resets on the first day of the next month.  

您可能会收到一条错误信息（例如 HTTP 429：请求过多），直到下个月第一天您的免费额度重置为止。

While the character count is the main limit, the free tier also has a few other restrictions when using the service.  

虽然字符数是主要的限制，但在使用该服务时，免费层级还有一些其他限制。

Feature    Free (F0) Tier Limit&#9;Description

Real-time requests&#9;20 transactions per 60 seconds&#9;This prevents you from sending too many requests at once.

Max audio length per request&#9;10 minutes&#9;    The audio generated from a single request cannot be longer than 10 minutes.

File size (in Audio Content Creation tool)&#9;3,000 characters per file&#9;The text you paste into a single file in the tool has this character limit.

Tips and Troubleshooting for Beginners  
 新手提示与故障排除

- **&quot;The first few words are missing!&quot;** Some users have reported that the generated audio occasionally cuts off the first word or two. This is sometimes a device-specific issue. If this happens, try playing the audio on a different device or in a different web browser to see if it resolves the problem.
“开头几个字不见了！” 有些用户反映，生成的音频偶尔会截断开头一两个字。这有时是设备特定的问题。如果遇到这种情况，可以尝试在其他设备或不同的网页浏览器中播放音频，看看是否能解决问题。
- **Choosing a Voice Region:** For the best performance and widest selection of voices, try to use a Speech resource in a major region like &#96;East US&#96;.
选择语音区域：为了获得最佳性能和最丰富的语音选择，请尽量在主要区域（如 &#96;East US&#96; ）使用语音资源。
- **Complexity of Azure:** One common piece of feedback from beginners is that the main Azure portal can feel overwhelming. Remember that for basic Text-to-Speech, you only need to use the portal for the initial setup. All your creative work can be done in the much simpler Speech Studio.
Azure 的复杂性：初学者常反馈称，主 Azure 门户界面可能会让人感到不知所措。请记住，对于基本的文本转语音功能，您只需在初始设置时使用该门户。所有创意工作都可以在更加简单的语音工作室中完成。
- **Known Issues:** Like any software, the service occasionally has minor bugs, such as specific words being pronounced incorrectly in certain languages. Microsoft maintains a list of [known issues](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-services/speech-service/known-issues) for the service, which can be useful if you encounter a persistent problem.
已知问题：与任何软件一样，该服务偶尔会出现一些小故障，例如在某些语言中特定单词的发音不准确。微软会维护一份该服务的已知问题列表，如果您遇到持续存在的问题，这份列表可能会有所帮助。
