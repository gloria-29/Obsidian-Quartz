List of things I had: 我拥有的东西清单：  

\- Fruit Juice \-果汁  
\- Tea with oat milk \-燕麦牛奶茶  
\- Coffee with oat milk 加燕麦奶的咖啡  
\- Store bought broths 市售的肉汤  
\- Homemade liquid soups 自制的液体汤羹  
\- Fruit/veggie smoothies 果蔬奶昔  
\- Liquid iron supplement 液体铁补充剂  
\- 3-4 Litres of water a day 每天 3 至 4 升水  
\- Monster drinks \-怪物饮料  
\- Zero cal pop drinks 零卡路里的汽水饮料  
\- Popsicles

I would never have a smoothie or soup on the same day since they were my main calorie source. So I’d have a smoothie one day, and a soup the next day. Total calories were approx under 500 on most days. If I had a smoothie and soup on the same day, 700.因为我把它们当作主要的热量来源，所以我绝不会在同一天喝奶昔和吃汤。所以我会一天喝奶昔，隔天吃汤。大多数日子的总热量都在 500 卡路里以下。如果同一天既喝奶昔又吃汤，热量就约 700 卡路里。

Drinking 3-4 Litres of water is such an important part of the fast. It kept me occupied with something to do and it was like having a mini goal everyday. Anytime I felt hungry I’d chug water.在禁食期间，每天喝 3 到 4 升水是如此重要。这让我有事可做，每天都有个小目标。每当感到饥饿时，我就大口喝水。

I wouldn’t drink juices as they came because they surprisingly have a lot of calories/sugar and it would slow down ketosis. I would instead carbonate them or dilute them with either Sprite Zero or water.我不会直接喝那些果汁，因为它们的热量/糖分出乎意料地高，这会减缓酮症的进程。我会选择把它们加气，或者用零度雪碧或水稀释。

If I went out and drank something with a lot of calories, I’d just cut the calories from next days intake.要是我出去喝了高热量的东西，那我就会在接下来的饮食中减少相应的热量摄入。

I added kale to my smoothies for iron. I also used coconut water for magnesium and potassium. Orange juice for vitamin c and calcium. I also had these iron supplements that were liquid but they ran out on day 8, but I survived.我在奶昔里加了羽衣甘蓝来补铁。我还用椰子水来补充镁和钾。橙汁来补充维生素 C 和钙。我还服用了这些液态的铁补充剂，但第八天就用完了，不过我还是挺过来了。

I did minimal exercise. I SOMETIMES did stretches. I walked to school and work so my commute would add up to 10 thousand steps. Majority of the time I was at home so I wasn’t doing much exercice.我几乎没怎么锻炼。有时会做做伸展运动。我步行去上学和上班，这样一天下来大概能走一万步。大多数时间我都在家，所以锻炼得不多。

I lost majority of my weight in the first few weeks. I was losing 2 pounds a day consistently. It was pretty noticeable after week 2. However, it did slow down so I did a day of water fasting to boost results here and there.我在最初的几周内减掉了大部分体重。我每天稳定减重 2 磅。两周后效果就很明显了。不过后来减重速度变慢了，所以我偶尔会进行一天的水断食来加快减重效果。

My only rules were: 我唯一的原则是：  

\- First thing I do in the day is drink water, last thing I do in the day is drink water. 我一天中做的第一件事是喝水，一天中做的最后一件事也是喝水。  
\- I have to dilute or carbonate sugary fruit juices 我得把含糖的果汁稀释一下或者加点碳酸。  
\- Drink 3-4 litres of water a day 每天饮用 3 至 4 升水
