### [](https://linux.do/t/topic/757481#p-6934320-h-4)怎么用？


1. 重中之重的是，你要学会自己创建账号
2. 把你自己创建的公司域名记下来，如示例中的 &#96;watchingforlinuxdo.retool.com&#96;  

[![image](https://linux.do/uploads/default/optimized/4X/e/d/8/ed804bcada9d2fd01a0b8dd826c6d25cbbca30ad_2_345x46.png)](https://linux.do/uploads/default/original/4X/e/d/8/ed804bcada9d2fd01a0b8dd826c6d25cbbca30ad.png)
3. 把两个鉴权 &#96;accessToken&#96; 和 &#96;xsrfToken&#96; 存下来（一周存活期）  

[![image](https://linux.do/uploads/default/optimized/4X/b/b/f/bbf86b236b7a7d99ef616195a503e70d78a4c7f1_2_345x232.png)](https://linux.do/uploads/default/original/4X/b/b/f/bbf86b236b7a7d99ef616195a503e70d78a4c7f1.png)
4. 保存到 &#96;retool.json&#96; 中，如下：



&#96;&#96;&#96;
[
    {
        &quot;domain_name&quot;: &quot;xxxx.retool.com&quot;,
        &quot;x_xsrf_token&quot;: &quot;751291ed-xxxx&quot;,
        &quot;accessToken&quot;: &quot;eyJhbGcixxxx&quot;
    }
]
&#96;&#96;&#96;



1. 

去 &#96;Agents(Beta)&#96; 页面创建两个新的 Agent，名为 &#96;claude-opus-4&#96; 和 &#96;claude-sonnet-4&#96;，并在设置页面按需配置模型  


[![image](https://linux.do/uploads/default/optimized/4X/a/c/a/aca81cc9cb1fbf0715130d1fb9ea70f079a030a6_2_345x236.png)](https://linux.do/uploads/default/original/4X/a/c/a/aca81cc9cb1fbf0715130d1fb9ea70f079a030a6.png)  

[![image](https://linux.do/uploads/default/optimized/4X/d/4/e/d4e66e3bb833337071068ed300bf47af6b95a0c2_2_345x236.png)](https://linux.do/uploads/default/original/4X/d/4/e/d4e66e3bb833337071068ed300bf47af6b95a0c2.png)



2. 

然后去一键运行代码  

[GitHub - oDaiSuno/retool2API](https://github.com/oDaiSuno/retool2API)
